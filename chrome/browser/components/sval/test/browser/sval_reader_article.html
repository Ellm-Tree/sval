Morning in the harbour town

The harbour town wakes before the light does. Fishing boats leave in a slow line past the breakwater, their engines low, while the bakeries on the front street pull the first trays of bread from ovens that have been warm since midnight. By the time the sun clears the hills, the market is already half set up. Paragraph 1 of the article continues the account at some length, so the page reads as prose.
