When you take control, the agent pauses and its Workspace is yours to use.
When you're done, you give the Workspace back and the agent carries on.

An agent is booking a table and reaches a page that asks you to prove
you're human. You take control, finish the check, and hand the Workspace
back.

<Screenshot name="features-agent" alt="A banner saying Claude is controlling this Workspace, with Take control and Stop agent buttons" />

## Steps

1. In the agent's window, select **Take control**.
2. Use the tabs as you would your own.
3. Above the page, select **Give Workspace back to** and the agent's name.

While you're in control, the bar above the page says **You control this
Workspace.**

## Details

- Until you take control, your clicks and key presses stay out of the
  agent's tabs. Your media keys play and pause your own media. The agent's
  pages get no input from a game controller, and they open no
  picture-in-picture window.
- When you take control, you control the whole Workspace in every window
  that shows it. That includes every tab in the Workspace and every tab you
  open there.
- Taking control ends what the agent was doing. The agent can't act
  anywhere in the Workspace until you give the Workspace back.
- While you're in control, the tabs behave as your own. Sval doesn't ask
  about sites, and your downloads and file choosers work as usual.
- In the Launcher, choose **Give Workspace back** to do the same as the
  button.
- You keep control after you restart Sval, until you give the Workspace
  back.
- An agent can't complete a check that asks whether you're human. When a
  page asks for that check, the card in the agent's window tells you and
  asks you to take control.
- A Quick Peek you open in the agent's window belongs to the agent too, so the
  agent can read and use it.
- To leave an agent's window without taking control, choose one of your
  own Workspaces from the Workspaces menu in the menu bar. The Workspace
  opens in a new window, and the agent keeps its window.
