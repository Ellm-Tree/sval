---
title: Unpair an AI app
description: Disconnect an AI app from Sval and delete its site rules.
---

When you unpair an app, Sval disconnects the app and deletes its
permissions. To use Sval again, the app has to ask you again.

You've stopped using Cursor and don't want it able to reach Sval.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Agents.
2. Under Paired agents, find the app, then select **Unpair**.
3. Optional: in the dialog, clear the box that starts with **Also delete
   its** to keep the app's Workspaces and their tabs. The box is checked each
   time the dialog opens.
4. Select **Unpair**.

The app disconnects at once. Settings says the app is unpaired and its
connection is closed.

## Details

- Unpairing deletes the app's site rules and other permissions.
- Sval deletes a Workspace you keep once no session has used it for the
  time set in Settings › Agents, as it does a paired app's Workspace. You
  can also delete it yourself.
- The app's own page under **Permissions** also has an Unpair button.
- Turning off agent access keeps your paired apps and their permissions.
  Unpairing removes them.
- To stop the app from even trying to reach Sval, go to Connect an AI app
  and select **Disconnect** next to the app. Then restart the app.
