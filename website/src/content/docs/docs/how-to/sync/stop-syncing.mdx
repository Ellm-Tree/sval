When you stop syncing, this device stops using the sync folder. Your Workspaces stay on
the device as they are.

You're selling an old laptop and want the laptop to stop reading your sync folder
before you wipe it.

## Steps

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

1. Open Settings › Sync and devices.
2. Under Sync, select **Stop Syncing**.
3. In the dialog, select **Stop syncing**.

The Sync card says "Sync is off" and offers the ways to choose a folder.

</Since>

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

1. Open Settings › Sync and devices.
2. Under Sync, select **Stop syncing**.

The Sync card reads "Sval saves your Workspaces on this device only. To sync
them, choose a folder your devices share."

</Until>

## Details

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- Sval keeps every Workspace on this computer and forgets the sign-in for an
  SMB server or Dropbox.
- Your other devices keep syncing with each other through the folder.
- This computer keeps the sync key. To sync again, choose the same folder
  with **A folder on this computer…**.

</Since>

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- Sval doesn't ask first. It keeps every Workspace on this device.
- Your other devices go on syncing with each other through the folder.
- This device keeps its sync key. To sync again, choose the same folder
  with **Choose folder…**.

</Until>

- Stopping sync doesn't stop a device you no longer have from reading the
  folder. To shut a lost device out, see
  [Set up sync again with a new key](/docs/how-to/sync/set-up-sync-again/).
