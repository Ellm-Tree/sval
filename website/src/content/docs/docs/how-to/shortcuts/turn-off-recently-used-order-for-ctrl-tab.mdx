<kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Tab</kbd> on Windows and Linux) switches to the tab you used last in
this Workspace. You can make the shortcut go to the next tab in the Workspace
instead.

You think of your tabs in the order the Workspace lists them, and want <kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> to
walk down that list.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Keyboard shortcuts.
2. In the Workspace panel group, turn off the switch for <kbd>Ctrl+Tab</kbd> switches to recently used tabs.

On a Mac, the switch reads <kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> switches to recently used tabs. With the
switch off, <kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> goes to the next tab in the Workspace.

## Details

- With the switch on, keep holding <kbd>⌃</kbd> (Ctrl) after <kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> to see a grid of every tab in the Workspace, the most recently used first. The grid opens when the Workspace has three or more tabs. Press Tab again to go further back through the grid.
- With the switch on or off, <kbd>⌃⇧Tab</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Tab</kbd>) goes to the previous tab in the Workspace while the grid isn't open.
- The setting syncs to your other devices.
