As you type, the Launcher lists what matches in sections: Open tabs, Pins
and Favorites, Commands, History, and Search suggestions. You choose the
order of the sections, how many rows each one shows, and which ones show at
all.

You switch between tabs far more often than you search, so you want more
open tabs and no search suggestions.

<Screenshot name="docs-launcher-sections" frame="rounded" maxWidth={600} alt="Settings › Search and Launcher › Launcher sections. Each section has a row count and buttons to move or hide it." />

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Search and Launcher.
2. Under Launcher sections, find the section you want to change.
3. In the menu next to the section's name, choose how many rows it shows.
4. To move the section, select **Move up** or **Move down**.
5. To take the section out of the Launcher, select **Hide**.

The Launcher uses the new order and limits the next time you type in it.

## Details

- A hidden section moves to the end of the list, marked **Not shown**.
  Select **Show** to put it back, last in the order.
- Choosing **None** keeps a section in its place with no rows.
- To start with, Open tabs shows 4 rows and History shows 5. Pins and
  Favorites, Commands, and Search suggestions show 3 rows each.
- The limits apply while you type. With nothing typed, or in a command's
  list of choices, the Launcher lists every row that fits.
- The first row is the one Enter opens. It always shows, whatever you
  hide.
- These settings sync to your other devices.
- The home page's search field follows the same settings.
