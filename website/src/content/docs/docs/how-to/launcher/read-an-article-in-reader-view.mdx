Reader View shows an article's text and pictures in a plain layout, without
the site's menus and ads.

You open a long article and want to read it without the sidebars.

<Screenshot name="docs-reader-view" alt="The Ink & Switch essay on local-first software in Reader View, with the Reader View toolbar to the left of the text" />

## Steps

1. On the article, select the Reader View button next to the address bar.

To leave Reader View, select the button again.

## Details

- The button shows only on a page Sval can read as an article.
- The keyboard shortcut is <kbd>⌥⌘R</kbd> on macOS, <kbd>F9</kbd> on Windows, and <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+R</kbd> on Linux.
- The Launcher's Reader view command does the same.
- In a narrow window, the button moves into the » menu.
