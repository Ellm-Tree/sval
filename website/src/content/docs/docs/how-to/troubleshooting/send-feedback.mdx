Send Feedback opens a form for your message. The form lists everything
your report would include. Before you send, you can view or uncheck each
part.

Pages in your Work Workspace stopped loading after an update, and you want
to tell Sval's developers what you were doing.

<Screenshot name="docs-send-feedback" frame="rounded" maxWidth={880} alt="The Send Feedback form with a description typed, next to the list of what Sval sends with it" />

## Steps

1. At the bottom of the Workspace panel, select **Send Feedback**.
2. Type your message.
3. Optional: type your email in **Email (optional)**, if you want a reply.
4. Under Sent with your feedback, uncheck any part you don't want to send.
5. Select **Send**.

The form says **Thanks, your feedback was sent.** and gives a reference.
Select **Close**.

## Details

- You can also open the form from the menu bar, with
  **Help › Send Feedback…**.
- **Send** stays dimmed until your message is at least 10 characters long.
- To see exactly what a part sends, select **View** on that part. To go
  back to the list, select **Back**.
- Sval always sends the part called Sval version and your system. The other
  parts start checked, except crash snapshots, which start unchecked.
- A crash snapshot can include parts of pages you had open. Send one only
  if you're comfortable with that.
- If a part would make the report bigger than 25 MB, the part says
  **Too large to send**.
- Nothing in the report identifies you unless you add your email. Sval's
  server doesn't keep your IP address.
- If you close the form with Esc, or by clicking outside the form, Sval
  keeps your draft until you quit Sval. To throw the draft away, select
  **Cancel**.
- Some error messages have a **Send Report…** button. The button opens the
  same form, with a line about the error.
- If you have a GitLab account, you can also report a problem in Sval's
  [issue tracker](https://gitlab.com/sval-browser/sval/-/issues), where
  everyone can read it and follow the fix.

## If something goes wrong

- **Couldn't reach Sval's feedback server.** Check your internet
  connection, and any proxy or firewall, then select **Send Again**.
- **You're offline.** Connect to the internet, then select **Send Again**.
  Sval keeps your message and choices.
- **Sval's feedback server had a problem.** The problem is on Sval's side.
  Try again in a few minutes.
- **Couldn't gather the report.** Select **Try Again**.
