You can select several rows in the Workspace and act on all of them at once.

You opened a dozen Fastmail search results in tabs, and you're done with
four of them.

<Screenshot name="docs-several-tabs" frame="rounded" maxWidth={500} alt="Three tabs selected in the Engineering group, with the menu for the selection: Move to group, New group with selection, Move to Workspace, and Close" />

## Steps

1. In the Workspace, <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) each row you
   want. To select every row in a range, click the first row, then
   Shift-click the last.
2. Right-click one of the selected rows.
3. Choose what to do with the selected rows: **Close**, **Move to group**,
   **New group with selection**, or **Move to Workspace**.

## Details

- <kbd>⌘</kbd>-clicking a selected row takes it out of the selection.
- Selecting a row this way doesn't show the row's page. The page on screen
  stays as it was.
- You can drag the selected rows together. They land where you drop them,
  in the same order. A dragged selection never makes a group or a Split.
- **Close** works only when every selected row is a tab. To remove a group,
  use **Delete group…** on the group.
- When you close several tabs, **Undo** shows at the bottom of the Workspace panel for
  8 seconds.
- While several rows are selected, the other entries in the menu, such as
  **Rename**, are dimmed.
- From the keyboard, <kbd>Shift+↑</kbd> and <kbd>Shift+↓</kbd> add rows to the selection.
  <kbd>Ctrl+Space</kbd> adds or removes the focused row. Escape clears the selection.
- Clicking a row without <kbd>⌘</kbd> or Shift clears the selection.
