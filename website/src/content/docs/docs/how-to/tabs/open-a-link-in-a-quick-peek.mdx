A Quick Peek opens a link on top of the page you're reading, without adding
a tab. Close the Quick Peek to go back, or keep the page if you want it.

A Quick Peek stays inside your window. A link from another app, like Slack
or Mail, opens in a [Peek](/docs/how-to/links/open-a-peek-in-a-workspace/),
a small window of its own.

<Screenshot name="home-quick-peek" alt="A Codeberg profile open as a Quick Peek over the pinned Codeberg tab, with Open in Split and Open as tab buttons" />

You're reading a Fastmail thread with a link to a Codeberg issue, and you
want a quick look before you answer.

## Steps

1. On the page, <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click (Alt+click on Windows and Linux) the link.
2. Read the page in the Quick Peek.
3. Press Escape to close the Quick Peek.

To open a Quick Peek from a link's menu, right-click the link, then choose
**Open Link in Quick Peek**.

## Keep the page

In the bar at the top of the Quick Peek:

- **Open as tab.** The page becomes a tab next to the one it came from, and
  Sval shows the new tab.
- **Open in Split.** The page shows next to the tab it came from, in a
  Split. If that tab is already in a Split, the page joins that Split. A
  Split of four pages has no room, and the button says so.

## Details

- The Quick Peek uses the sign-ins of the tab it opened from.
- The bar shows the page's address.
- Clicking outside the Quick Peek or on its close button also closes it. So
  does switching to another tab, or the tab underneath going to another
  page.
- A window shows one Quick Peek at a time.
- A page you opened in a Quick Peek goes to History. It doesn't go to recently closed
  tabs.
- In a pin, a link to another site opens in a Quick Peek when you click it, with
  no <kbd>⌥</kbd> key needed. The bar then starts with "From" and the pin's name.
- You can open a Quick Peek with <kbd>⌥⌘</kbd>-click or <kbd>⇧⌥</kbd>-click
  (Ctrl+Alt+click or Shift+Alt+click on Windows and Linux) instead. Go to
  Settings › Keyboard shortcuts › Links, then select **Change** next to
  **Open a link in a Quick Peek**.
- To turn off Quick Peek, go to Settings › Links › Links in pages and turn
  off the first switch. Or select **Clear** next to **Open a link in a Quick
  Peek** in Settings › Keyboard shortcuts. The click then does what it does
  without Sval, and Open Link in Quick Peek leaves the link's menu. Links
  from pins have their own switch in Settings › Links: Open links to other
  sites from a pin in a Quick Peek.
