A private window keeps nothing after you close it. Each private window is a
session of its own.

<Screenshot name="features-private" alt="A Sval private window in violet, with a single New private tab and a note that nothing is kept or synced" />

You want to check a Codeberg page signed out, without signing out of your
own account.

## Steps

1. Press <kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+N</kbd> on Windows and Linux).

A private window opens. The top of its Workspace panel reads "Private window. Tabs
here aren't saved or synced."

In the menu bar, **File › New Private Window** does the same.

## Details

- A private window can't see the cookies or site data of another private
  window, or of your Workspaces.
- Nothing about a private window's tabs is saved to disk or synced.
- Choices you make for a site in a private window last until you close the
  window. Settings you open from a private window can't change anything
  Sval keeps, such as an extension's switch for a site.
- When you close a private window, Sval deletes the window's cookies and
  site data.
- When a page opens a sign-in window, the sign-in window shares the page's
  session, so signing in works.
- A private window has one Workspace of its own. It has no list of
  Workspaces, no Favorites, and no pins.
- Tabs and groups work as in any window.
- You can't move a tab into or out of a private window.
- Files you download stay on your computer.

- When a tab showing a website crashes in a private window, Sval keeps no
  crash report of that crash. While a private window is open, Sval also
  keeps no crash report when Sval itself or one of its background processes
  crashes.
