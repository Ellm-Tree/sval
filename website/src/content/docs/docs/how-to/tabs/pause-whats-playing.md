---
title: Pause what's playing
description: Pause or mute a tab, a group, or a Workspace, and use the media controls at the bottom of the Workspace panel.
---

While a tab plays sound, a pause button shows on the tab's row. The
media controls at the bottom of the Workspace panel act on whatever played
last in the window.

A video started playing somewhere in your tabs, and you want it quiet.

## Pause one tab

1. Click the pause button on the tab's row.

The video or music pauses. To play it again, click the button again.

You can also use the tab's menu. Right-click the row, or focus the row and
press <kbd>Shift+F10</kbd>. Then choose **Pause Media**. While the tab is
paused, the menu item reads **Play Media**.

## Use the media controls

While anything in the window has media, controls show at the bottom of the
Workspace panel:

- **Back 10 Seconds** and **Forward 10 Seconds**.
- Play and pause.
- **Previous Track** and **Next Track**, on sites that have tracks.

The controls act on whatever played last in this window, in any of the
window's Workspaces.

## Stop videos moving into Picture-in-Picture

When you switch to another tab or Workspace while a video plays, the video
keeps playing in a Picture-in-Picture window. Select the video's tab again,
and the window closes.

1. Go to Settings › General.
2. Under Media, turn off **Keep playing videos in Picture-in-Picture when
   switching tabs**.

## Details

- A folded group shows the pause button when a tab inside the group plays.
  The group's button pauses every tab in the group that's playing.
- In the list of Workspaces, a Workspace with sound shows the pause button
  too.
- Point to the button to see what's playing.
- When sound can't be paused, a mute button shows in place of the pause
  button. The menu then reads **Mute** or **Unmute**.
- Play starts again whatever the pause stopped.
- A video moves into Picture-in-Picture only when it has sound, is at least
  140 pixels wide and tall, and is at least 45 seconds long or a live stream.
