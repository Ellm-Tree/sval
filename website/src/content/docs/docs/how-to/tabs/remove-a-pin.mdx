<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

A pin is a Favorite that shows in the pins row. Unpinning takes the pin out
of the row, and you keep the Favorite. Deleting removes both the pin and the
Favorite.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

Moving a pin to Favorites takes the pin out of the pins row and puts its
site at the end of your Favorites. Deleting a pin removes it from the pins
row.

</Since>

You pinned a Codeberg project for a week of work on it, and the work is
done.

<Screenshot name="docs-pin-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={400} alt="The Roon pin's menu, with Unpin and Delete at the bottom" />

## Keep it as a Favorite

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

1. In the pins row, right-click the pin, then choose **Unpin**.

The pin leaves the row and goes back to its place in your Favorites. If the
pin's page was open, the page becomes a tab at the top of the list of tabs.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

1. In the pins row, right-click the pin, then choose **Move to Favorites**.

The pin leaves the row and goes to the end of your Favorites. If the pin's
page was open, the page becomes a tab at the top of the list of tabs.

</Since>

## Delete it

1. In the pins row, right-click the pin, then choose **Delete**.

The pin leaves the row, and its page closes. A message at the bottom of the
Workspace panel offers **Undo** for 8 seconds.

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

Once the message goes, Sval deletes the Favorite.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

Deleting a pin never deletes a Favorite with the same address.

</Since>

## Details

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- Unpin and Delete act on a pin in every Workspace and on your other
  devices.
- On a pinned folder, Unpin unpins the folder and everything in it. Delete
  deletes the folder and everything in it.
- A pin inside a pinned folder has no Unpin of its own. Delete removes that
  pin from the folder.
- To take back an Unpin, select Undo at the top of the Favorites menu.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- Move to Favorites and Delete act on a pin in every Workspace and on your
  other devices.
- While a pin is selected, the menu bar's Tab menu has
  **Move Pin to Favorites** too.
- When you move a pin to Favorites, Sval forgets which Workspaces showed
  the pin. Undo puts back the Workspaces the pin showed in.
- On a pin folder, Move to Favorites moves the pin folder and everything
  in it. Delete deletes the pin folder and everything in it. To keep the
  pins and lose the pin folder, choose **Move Contents to Row**.
- A pin inside a pin folder has no Move to Favorites of its own. Delete
  removes that pin from the pin folder.
- To take back a Move to Favorites, select **Undo** in the message at the
  bottom of the Workspace panel. The message stays for 8 seconds. The pin
  goes back to its place in the row, and its open page becomes the pin's
  page again.

</Since>
