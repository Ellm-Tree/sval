A pin shows in every Workspace until you pick the Workspaces it should show
in.

Your Roon pin belongs in Home, and you don't want it in Work.

<Screenshot name="docs-pin-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={400} alt="The Roon pin's menu with Show in open. Work, Home, and Ceramics Shop are checked." />

## Steps

1. In the pins row, right-click the pin, then point to **Show in**.
2. Check each Workspace the pin should show in, and uncheck the others.
3. Close the menu.

The pin leaves the pins row in every Workspace you unchecked.

## Details

- To check every Workspace, select **Select all**. To uncheck every
  Workspace, select **Deselect all**. The menu stays open while you check.
- With every Workspace checked, the pin also shows in Workspaces you make
  later.
- If you close the menu with nothing checked, the pin stays where it was.

- What you choose for a pin folder applies to every pin inside the pin
  folder. A pin inside a pin folder has no Show in of its own.

- To pin a tab only in the Workspace you're in, right-click the tab and
  choose **Pin to This Workspace**.
- Your choice of Workspaces for a pin syncs to your other devices.
- When you delete a Workspace, Sval takes that Workspace out of every pin's
  list. A pin left with no Workspaces shows in every Workspace.
- Show in never lists agent Workspaces.
- In a Workspace that doesn't show the pin, <kbd>⌘1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘9</kbd> skip that pin.
