You can put the Workspace panel on either side of the window. You can also
make it any width between its narrowest and widest.

You'd rather have your tabs on the right, next to your mouse.

<Screenshot name="docs-panel-side" frame="rounded" maxWidth={600} alt="Settings › Appearance › Workspace panel side, set to Left" />

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Appearance.
2. Under Workspace panel side, choose **Right** or **Left**.

The Workspace panel moves in every window.

## Change the Workspace panel's width

1. Drag the edge between the Workspace panel and the page.

To put the Workspace panel back to its default width, double-click the edge.

## Details

- The side and the width are kept on each device. They don't sync.
- The History sidebar opens on the side opposite the Workspace panel.
- From the keyboard, Tab to the Workspace panel's edge, then use the arrow
  keys to resize the Workspace panel. Home makes it narrowest, and End
  makes it widest. Enter puts back the default width.
- The Workspace panel's greatest width is a share of the window, so that width
  changes with the window's size.
