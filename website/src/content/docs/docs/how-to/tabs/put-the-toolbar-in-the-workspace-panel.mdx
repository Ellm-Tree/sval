You can move the address bar and its buttons into the Workspace panel,
above your tabs, so the page runs from the top of the window to the
bottom.

You read long documents on a laptop screen and want every line of height
for the page.

<Screenshot name="docs-toolbar-in-panel" frame="rounded" alt="The address bar and the toolbar buttons at the top of the Workspace panel, above the Workspaces. A Houzz page runs the full height of the window." />

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Appearance.
2. Under Workspace appearance, in **Toolbar**, choose **In the Workspace
   panel**.

To put the toolbar back, choose **Above the page**.

## Details

- Downloads and History move to the bottom of the Workspace panel next to
  Favorites, and so do Sval's notices.
- Clicking the address opens the Launcher.
- As you narrow the Workspace panel, the buttons that no longer fit move
  into the **More tools** button, and the site controls move into the
  site settings menu.
- At its narrowest, the Workspace panel shows one column of buttons, with
  the window's buttons stacked at the top.
- While the Workspace panel is hidden, the page's top corner keeps the
  window's buttons and a **Workspaces** button that shows the Workspace
  panel again. An icon shows next to the Workspaces button while a site
  uses your camera, microphone, or screen. A line along the top edge shows
  the page loading, and a site that asks for a permission slides the Workspace
  panel in until you answer.
- Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to reach the toolbar's buttons, and the arrow keys
  to move between them.
