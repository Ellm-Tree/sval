Keep Awake stops Sval from putting a tab to sleep. The tab's page keeps
running while you work in other Workspaces.

You keep a Roon tab running in Home, and want it to stay ready while you
spend the day in Work.

<Screenshot name="docs-tab-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={260} alt="A tab's menu in the Workspace, with Keep Awake near the bottom" />

## Steps

1. In the Workspace, right-click the tab, then choose **Keep Awake**.

A check mark shows next to Keep Awake in the tab's menu. If the tab was
asleep, its page loads.

To let the tab sleep again, choose **Keep Awake** again.

## Details

- A tab you keep awake stays loaded when its Workspace has been out of
  sight for hours. It also stays loaded when your computer runs low on memory.
- Put to Sleep leaves a tab you keep awake as it is.
- Keep Awake works on one tab at a time. A group's menu doesn't have it.
