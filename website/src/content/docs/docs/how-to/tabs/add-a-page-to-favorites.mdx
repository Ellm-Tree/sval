Favorites are the pages you keep. You'll find them in the Favorites menu at
the bottom of the Workspace panel. Favorites don't belong to any Workspace, so you can
open one from any Workspace.

You found a Codeberg page on release notes you'll come back to, but you
don't need it open.

<Screenshot name="docs-favorites-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={360} alt="The Favorites menu open above the Favorites button at the bottom of the Workspace panel, with the Reading and Tools folders and two Favorites" />

## Steps

1. In the Workspace, right-click the tab, then choose **Add to Favorites**.

Sval says "Added to Favorites". The page is now in the Favorites menu.

To take a page out of Favorites, right-click its tab and choose
**Remove from Favorites**.

## Pin a Favorite

1. At the bottom of the Workspace panel, select **Favorites**.

2. Right-click the Favorite, then choose **Pin**.

A copy of the Favorite shows at the end of the pins row. The Favorite stays
in the Favorites menu. Pin works on a folder too, and the folder's copy
shows in the pins row as one tile. If a pin already has the Favorite's
address, the menu doesn't offer Pin.

## Details

- <kbd>⌘D</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+D</kbd> on Windows and Linux) adds the page you're viewing. It never
  removes one. On a page that's already a Favorite, Sval says "Already in
  Favorites".
- The **Favorite** button in the toolbar adds the page you're viewing. If
  the page is already a Favorite, the button removes it.

- On a pin's page, the **Favorite** button shows whether the page is in
  Favorites. Adding or removing the Favorite leaves the pin as it is.

- <kbd>⇧⌘O</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+O</kbd> on Windows and Linux) shows the Favorites menu.
- In the Favorites menu, right-click a Favorite or a folder to find
  **Rename…**, **Edit Address…**, **Delete**, **Move to**, and
  **New Folder…**, among others. **Pin to This Workspace** pins the Favorite
  or folder only in the Workspace you're in.

- Favorites sync to your other devices, along with your Workspaces and
  groups.
- A private window has no Favorites menu, and you can't add to Favorites
  from one.
