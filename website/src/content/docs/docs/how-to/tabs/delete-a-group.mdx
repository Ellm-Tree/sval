Deleting a group removes the group from its Workspace. Sval asks whether the
group's tabs move out of the group or go with it.

Your release shipped, and the group of Codeberg issues you kept for it can
go.

<Screenshot name="docs-group-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={260} alt="The menu for the Kitchen reno group, with Delete group… at the bottom" />

## Steps

1. In the Workspace, right-click the group, then choose **Delete group…**.
2. Choose what happens to the tabs and groups inside it.
   - **Move out of group.** Sval keeps the tabs and groups that were
     inside. They take the group's place, in the same order.
   - **Delete all.** Sval closes every tab inside the group, and deletes
     the groups inside it too.

The group leaves the Workspace. A message at the bottom of the Workspace
panel offers **Undo** for 8 seconds.

## Details

- Sval deletes an empty group without asking.
- With the group's row focused, press Delete or Backspace to get the same
  question.
- In the dialog, **Cancel** has the focus, so pressing Enter or Escape keeps
  the group.
- Until the Undo message goes, your other devices still have the group.
  Once the message goes, Sval deletes the group everywhere.

## If something goes wrong

You deleted the wrong group. Select **Undo** in the message at the bottom of
the Workspace panel before the message goes. See
[Undo closing tabs or deleting a group](/docs/how-to/tabs/undo-closing-tabs-or-deleting-a-group/).
