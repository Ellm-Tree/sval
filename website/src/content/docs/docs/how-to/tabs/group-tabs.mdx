A group keeps tabs, and other groups, together under one name in the Workspace.
You can give a group a color and an emoji. You can also fold the group away
when you don't need it.

You have six Codeberg issues open for one release, and you want them in one
place you can fold away.

<Screenshot name="docs-group-tabs" frame="rounded" maxWidth={300} alt="A Workspace with nested groups: Research, tinted teal, inside Q4 Launch, with a Pricing pages group and a Split inside it, and Engineering and Hiring folded" />

## Make an empty group

1. At the top of the Workspace panel, select **New group**.
2. Type a name in **Name**.
3. Optional: choose an **Emoji** and a **Color**.
4. Select **Create**.

The group appears in the Workspace or group you're working in, after the
groups already there and before the tabs.

## Make a group from two tabs

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

1. Drag one tab's row onto the middle of another tab's row.
2. When the row reads **New group**, drop the tab.
3. In the New group dialog, type a name, then select **Create**.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

1. Drag one tab's row onto the left half of another tab's row.
2. When the **New group** half fills with color, drop the tab.
3. In the New group dialog, type a name, then select **Create**.

</Since>

The new group takes the place of the tab you dropped on, with both tabs
inside.

## Make a group from several tabs

1. In the Workspace, select the rows. <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux)
   to add a row, or Shift-click to add every row in between.
2. Right-click one of the selected rows, then choose **New group with selection**.
3. Type a name, then select **Create**.

## Details

- To put a tab in a group, drag the tab onto a folded group's row, or among
  an open group's tabs. You can also right-click the tab and choose
  **Move to group**. From the keyboard, focus the tab's row and press
  <kbd>Alt+→</kbd> to move the tab into the group above. <kbd>Alt+←</kbd>
  moves the tab back out.
- A group can hold other groups. Drag one group onto another, or use
  **Move to group** on the group you're moving.
- To make a group inside another, right-click the outer group, then choose
  **New group**.
- Click a group's row to fold or unfold the group. A folded group shows how
  many tabs it holds. While you view a tab from a folded group, that tab
  shows under the group's row.
- To change a group's name, color, or emoji, right-click the group and
  choose **Edit…**. To rename the group, double-click its name, or press
  <kbd>F2</kbd>.
- A group with a color is drawn as a tinted block around its tabs.
- A group needs a name. **Create** won't make a group while the name is
  empty.
- Groups sync to your other devices along with your Workspaces. Each device
  keeps track of which groups are folded on that device.
- If you drop a tab on the right end of another tab's row, you get a Split
  instead of a group. See
  [Show tabs side by side in a Split](/docs/how-to/tabs/show-tabs-side-by-side-in-a-split/).
