---
title: See history from every Workspace
description: Switch History from the pages of the Workspace you're in to the pages of every Workspace.
---

History lists the pages you visited in the Workspace you're in. You can
switch History to list the pages from all your Workspaces.

You read a Fastmail help page last week, and you can't remember which
Workspace you were in.

## Steps

1. In the toolbar, select **History**.
2. At the top of the History sidebar, select **All Workspaces**.
3. Optional: type part of the page's title or address in the search box.

Each page now shows the Workspace it was last visited in.

## Details

- History opens next to the page, on the side opposite the Workspace panel. <kbd>⇧⌘H</kbd>
  (<kbd>Ctrl+H</kbd> on Windows and Linux) also opens History.
- **This Workspace.** History lists the pages visited in this Workspace,
  and pages visited outside any Workspace.
- In each window, History starts on This Workspace. History goes back to
  This Workspace when the window switches to another Workspace.
- To clear history, select **Clear Recent History…** at the bottom of the
  sidebar.
- A private window records no history. In a private window, History lists
  your history from every Workspace.
