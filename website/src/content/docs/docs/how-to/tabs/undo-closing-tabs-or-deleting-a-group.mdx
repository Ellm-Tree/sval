When you close several tabs at once, or delete a group, a pin, or a
Workspace, a message at the bottom of the Workspace panel offers
**Undo** for 8 seconds. Undo puts everything back where it was.

You closed four Codeberg tabs together, and one of them still had a review
you hadn't sent.

<Screenshot name="docs-undo-message" frame="rounded" maxWidth={280} alt="Closed 2 tabs, with an Undo button, at the bottom of the Workspace panel" />

## Steps

1. At the bottom of the Workspace panel, in the message such as "Closed 4 tabs",
   select **Undo** before the message goes.

The tabs, group, pin, or Workspace come back where they were.

## Details

- <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> on Windows and Linux) also undoes while the message shows,
  unless the page or a text field has the focus. The page and text fields
  keep <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> for their own undo.
- The message stays while the pointer is on the message, or while the
  message has the focus.
- To close the message and make the change for good, select **Dismiss**
  next to Undo.
- You can undo only the latest change. A new close or delete replaces the
  message, and the change before it is made for good.
- If you close the window or quit Sval while the message shows, the change
  is made for good.
- Your other devices hear about the change only once the message goes.
- Closing a single tab shows no message. <kbd>⇧⌘T</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+T</kbd> on Windows and
  Linux) reopens the tab you closed last.
