---
title: Keep a site's links in the same tab
description: Stop a site from opening its own pages in new tabs.
since: 0.1.0-rc.11
---

Keep links in this tab stops a site from opening its own pages in new tabs or
windows. A link from one of the site's pages to another page of the same
site loads in the tab you're on. Back returns you to where you were.

You browse eBay listings, and each one you open lands in a new tab that you
then have to close.

## Steps

1. On the site, select the **Site settings** button in the address bar.
2. Under Links, turn on **Keep links in this tab**.

The site's next link that asks for a new tab opens in the tab you're on
instead. The tab doesn't reload.

## Details

- A link to another site still opens a new tab.
- A link you open with Command-click on a Mac, Ctrl-click on Windows or
  Linux, or a middle-click still opens in a new tab.
- A site's sign-in and payment windows still open, because the site asks for
  them with a window size.
- Your choice syncs to your other devices, the iPhone app included.
- On an address like `shop.example.com`, the switch covers that address
  alone. To cover all of `example.com`, pick All of example.com in the
  menu's title.
- You can't change Keep links in this tab in a private window, because Sval
  saves and syncs the choice.
- The site's page in Settings › Sites has the same switch.
