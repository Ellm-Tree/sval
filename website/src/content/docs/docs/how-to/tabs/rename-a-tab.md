---
title: Rename a tab
description: Give a tab a name of your own that it keeps as you browse, and show the page's title again.
---

A tab shows its page's title until you give it a name of your own. The tab
keeps your name as you browse, on every device you sync.

You keep a tab open on your team's dashboard, whose title changes with each
page. You'd rather the tab always read "Dashboard".

## Steps

1. In the Workspace, right-click the tab.
2. Choose **Rename**.
3. In the Rename Tab dialog, type the name, then select **Rename**.

Double-clicking the tab's name opens the same dialog, and so does
<kbd>F2</kbd> while the tab's row has focus.

## Show the page's title again

1. Right-click the tab.
2. Choose **Remove Name**.

Clearing the name in the Rename Tab dialog does the same.

## Details

- In the Launcher, **Rename this tab** and **Remove this tab’s name** act
  on the tab you're on.
- Remove Name shows only on a tab you've renamed.
