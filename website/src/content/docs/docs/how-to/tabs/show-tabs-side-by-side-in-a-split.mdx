A Split shows up to four tabs side by side in one window. In the Workspace, a
Split is one row with a tile for each tab.

You're comparing two laptops on two shop pages and want both in view.

<Screenshot name="docs-split" alt="Linear's pricing page and Vercel's pricing page side by side in a Split, with the Split's row in the Pricing pages group" />

## Split two tabs you have open

1. In the Workspace, select the first tab.
2. Option-click (Alt-click on Windows and Linux) the second tab's row.

The two tabs show side by side, with the one you were viewing on the left.

## Split a tab with one you pick

1. In the Workspace, right-click the tab, then choose **Create Split**.
2. In the new pane, choose the tab to show next to the first one.

<kbd>⌃+</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Plus</kbd> on Windows and Linux) does the same for the tab you're
viewing. On a US keyboard, <kbd>⌃+</kbd> is <kbd>⌃⇧=</kbd>.

## Open a link in a Split

1. On the page, right-click the link, then choose **Open Link in Split View**.

The link opens next to the page you're on.

## Details

- A Split holds up to four tabs. While you view a Split that has room,
  Option-click another tab to add it as the rightmost pane. <kbd>⌃+</kbd>
  also adds a pane, and you pick the tab to show in it.
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- You can also drag one tab's row onto the right end of another tab's row.
  While you're over that spot, the row reads **Create Split**. Over a
  Split's row, it reads **Join Split**. When you drag a Split, it reads
  **Merge Splits**. A narrow Workspace panel has no such spot.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- You can also drag one tab's row onto the right half of another tab's row.
  While you're over that half, it fills with color and reads **Create
  Split**. Over a Split's row, the half reads **Join Split**. When you
  drag a Split, the half reads **Merge Splits**. In a narrow Workspace
  panel, the row shows only the **New group** half.

</Since>

- The new pane lists the tabs of this Workspace that aren't in a Split.
- To add a tab to a Split in this Workspace that has room, choose
  **Add to Split** in the tab's menu.
- Drag the divider between two panes to resize them. Sval keeps the sizes
  after a restart. The sizes sync to your other devices along with the
  Split.
- Right-click a pane's tile in the Workspace to find **Move Pane Left**,
  **Move Pane Right**, **Rename Split…**, **Separate**, and **Close All**.
  In a Split of two tabs, Close All reads **Close Both**. <kbd>Alt+←</kbd>
  and <kbd>Alt+→</kbd> also move the focused pane.
- **Separate.** The Split turns back into ordinary tabs, in the same place.
- When you close one tab in a Split of two, the other tab is separated.
- A Split without a name shows the titles of its pages. To name a Split,
  use **Rename Split…**. An empty name brings back the titles.
- In a Quick Peek, **Open in Split** shows the page next to the tab it came from.
- Panes only sit side by side. Sval has no top-and-bottom or grid layout.
- You can't pin a tab in a Split.
