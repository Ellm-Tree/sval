Your Sval Card has a title and a star for every year you've browsed with
Sval. If you used Arc and Arc gave you a card, your Sval Card carries that
card's title and years across.

You've used Sval for a while and want to see how many Workspaces and pins
you've built up.

<Screenshot name="docs-sval-card" frame="rounded" maxWidth={560} alt="The Sval Card titled The Calm Lookout. Next to the card are the date you started using Sval, the date of the next star, and counts of Workspaces, open tabs, Favorites, pins, devices that have synced, rules in My filters, and trackers and ads blocked." />

## Steps

1. In the menu bar, choose View › **Your Sval Card**.

You can also select **Your Sval Card** at the bottom of the Settings
sidebar, or select your card in About Sval.

## Save a copy

1. Open your card.
2. Select **Save image**.

Sval saves your card as a PNG in your downloads folder.

## Details

- The card shows the day you started with Sval and the date of your next
  star.
- Next to your card, Sval counts what you've built up, from your
  Workspaces and open tabs to your Favorites, pins, and rules in My
  filters.
  Sval also counts the devices that have synced and the trackers and ads
  Sval has blocked on this computer.
- Your card is the same on every device you sync.
- When you import from Arc and Arc gave you a card, your card keeps the
  title Arc gave you. The stars for your Arc years come first, each in your
  Arc card's shape. Sval mentions Arc on your card only when you have an Arc
  card.
