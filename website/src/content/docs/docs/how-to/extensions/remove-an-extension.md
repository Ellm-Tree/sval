---
title: Remove an extension
description: Delete an extension and its data from Sval, or turn it off and keep it.
---

When you remove an extension, Sval deletes the extension and its data from
this device. If you turn the extension off instead, Sval keeps it, ready to
turn on again.

You tried a reading-list extension and don't use it.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Extensions.
2. Under Installed, select the extension's name.
3. Under Remove, select **Remove**.
4. In the dialog, select **Remove**.

Sval says **Removed** and the extension's name.

## Details

- To keep the extension but stop it from running, turn it off. Use the
  extension's switch in the Installed list, or turn off **On** on the
  extension's page.
- Removing an extension on this device doesn't remove it on your other
  devices.
- If Sval can't remove an extension, the extension's page has no Remove
  card.
