---
title: Turn an extension off on a site
description: Stop an extension from reading or changing anything on one site, and keep it working everywhere else.
---

Each extension has a switch for each site. When you turn an extension off
on a site, the extension can't read or change anything in tabs showing
that site. Everywhere else, the extension works as before.

A page-styling extension breaks the layout of your Roon dashboard, and you
want it to leave that one site alone.

<Screenshot name="features-extensions" frame="rounded" maxWidth={480} alt="The extensions menu on theverge.com. Each extension shows what it can do on the site, with a Run on theverge.com switch." />

## From the extensions menu

1. Go to a page on the site.
2. In the toolbar, select **Extensions**.
3. On the extension's row, turn off the **Run on** switch.

Sval reloads the site's tabs. The extension's row then says the extension can't
see or change anything on the site.

## From Settings

1. Open Settings › Extensions.
2. Under Installed, select the extension's name.
3. Under Sites where this extension is off, type the site in **Turn it off on a site**.
4. Select **Turn off**.

## Details

- Each row in the extensions menu says what that extension can do on the
  site you're on. A row can list reading and changing the site's pages,
  seeing the site's connections, or reading and changing the site's
  cookies.
- When an extension is off on a site, it can't read or change the site's
  pages or cookies. The extension can't see the site's connections, or see
  you open the site's pages.
- Some addresses have a part before the site's name, such as
  app.fastmail.com. On a page like that, the switch starts out covering
  that address alone. To switch the whole site, choose
  **All of fastmail.com** in the Run on line at the top of the menu.
- To turn the extension back on, turn its switch back on in the menu, or
  next to the site in Settings.
- In Settings › Sites, each site's page lists the extensions that can
  reach that site, with a switch for each one.
- Your switches sync to your other devices.
- In a private window, the extensions menu doesn't show the switches. If
  you open Settings in a private window, you'll see the switches but can't
  change them, because your switches are saved and synced.
- On pages without a site, such as Settings, the menu doesn't show the
  switches either.
- The menu shows a switch only for an extension that can reach the site.
