---
title: Sign in to Kagi
description: Sign in to Kagi so your searches work in normal windows, and let private windows search with Privacy Pass.
---

Kagi is a paid search engine, so you need to be signed in to search with
Kagi. In normal windows, Sval signs in with your Kagi account. In private
windows, Sval signs in with Privacy Pass. Privacy Pass shows Kagi you're a
subscriber without saying which one.

You chose Kagi when you set up Sval, but skipped signing in.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Search and Launcher.
2. On the Kagi card, select **Sign in to Kagi**.
3. In the window that opens, sign in to your Kagi account.

The card reads Signed in to Kagi. Under it, a line says how private
windows sign in.

## If your plan has no Privacy Pass

Some Kagi plans don't include Privacy Pass. If yours doesn't, the card
says private windows are signed out. The card also shows a Kagi Session
Link field.

1. In Kagi's settings, copy your Session Link.
2. On the Kagi card, paste the Session Link into **Kagi Session Link**.
3. Select **Save**.

The line under the field reads Saved. From then on, private windows search
with your Session Link. A Session Link tells Kagi who you are, so Kagi
links your private searches to your account.

## Details

- The Kagi card shows while Kagi is your default search engine.
- You can also sign in when you first set up Sval. Choose Kagi, then select
  **Sign in to Kagi** under Kagi.
- Each device signs in on its own. Your sign-in, Privacy Pass searches, and
  Session Link never sync.
- Each private search uses up one Privacy Pass search. The card says how
  many you have left. When you've used this month's, the card says the
  date they reset.
- If you change plans, select **Check again** to have Sval ask Kagi again
  whether your plan includes Privacy Pass.
- To remove a Session Link, select **Clear**.

## If something goes wrong

- **That's not a Kagi Session Link. Copy it again from Kagi.** The text
  you pasted isn't a Session Link. Copy the whole link from Kagi and paste
  it again.
