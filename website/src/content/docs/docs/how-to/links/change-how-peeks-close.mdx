A Peek you don't use closes after 6 hours. You can choose another time, keep
Peeks until you close them, or turn Peeks off.

You leave a Peek with a recipe open on your second screen all day, and you
don't want it to close while you cook.

<Screenshot name="docs-links-from-other-apps" frame="rounded" maxWidth={600} alt="Settings › Links › Links from other apps, with Open links from other apps in a Peek turned on and Close an idle Peek after set to 6 hours" />

## Change when a Peek closes

1. Open Settings › Links.
2. Under Links from other apps, choose a time in **Close an idle Peek after**.

You can choose 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, or 1 week. Choose **Never** to keep
a Peek open until you close it.

## Turn Peeks off

1. Open Settings › Links.
2. Under Links from other apps, turn off **Open links from other apps in a Peek**.

From then on, links from other apps open in a new tab in your frontmost
window.

## Details

- A Peek counts as used while you're in its window. The time keeps
  counting while your computer sleeps.
- When a Peek closes by itself, its page stays in History.
- Whatever time you choose, Peeks still close when Sval quits.
- Both settings sync to your other devices.
- Link Docking's rules apply whether Peeks are on or off.
  See [Dock a site's links in a Workspace](/docs/how-to/links/send-a-sites-links-to-a-workspace/).
