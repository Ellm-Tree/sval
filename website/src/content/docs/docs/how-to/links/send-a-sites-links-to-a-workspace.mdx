With Link Docking, links from other apps that match a rule dock in the
rule's Workspace as a new tab, without a Peek.

Every Fastmail link you open from your calendar belongs in your Home
Workspace. You add a Link Docking rule, and those links open there without a Peek.

<Screenshot name="docs-link-rule-form" frame="rounded" maxWidth={600} alt="A new rule in Settings › Links › Link Docking that docks fastmail.com links in the Home Workspace, with the line that says what the rule will do" />

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Links.
2. Under Link Docking, select **Add rule**.
3. In **Match by**, choose **Site** or **Address contains**.
4. Type the site, like fastmail.com, or part of an address, like
   codeberg.org/acme.
5. In **Workspace**, choose the Workspace.
6. Select **Add**.

The line under the form says what the rule will do before you add it, like
"Links from other apps to fastmail.com and its subdomains dock in Home
Workspace."

## Change or remove a rule

- To change a rule, select the pencil next to it, make your change, then
  select **Save**.
- To delete a rule, select the × next to it. To get the rule back, select
  **Undo**.
- To change the order, drag a rule up or down. With the keyboard, move
  focus to the rule's grip, then press the Up or Down arrow key.

## Details

- **Site.** The rule matches the site and its subdomains. You can paste a
  whole address, and Sval keeps the site and drops the rest.
- **Address contains.** The rule matches any address with that text in it.
  The text needs at least 3 characters and no spaces.
- Sval tries the rules in order, and the first rule that matches wins. If a
  rule above always matches first, the rule below it says
  **This rule never applies.**
- The link opens as a new tab in the window that shows the Workspace. If no
  window shows the Workspace, your frontmost window switches to it.
- Rules apply whether Peeks are on or off.
- Rules apply only to links from other apps. Links you click in Sval aren't
  changed.
- Rules sync to all your devices. While sync is off, they stay on this
  device.
- Agent Workspaces aren't in the **Workspace** list.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Workspace.** The Workspace the rule sent links to was deleted. Edit
  the rule and choose another Workspace. Until you do, links open as if the
  rule weren't there.
- **Rule 2 is already for fastmail.com.** A rule for that site already
  exists. Edit that one instead.
