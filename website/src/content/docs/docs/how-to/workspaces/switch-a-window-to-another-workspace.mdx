Each window shows one Workspace, with the Workspace's name above its tabs.
When you switch the window to another Workspace, the window shows that Workspace's
tabs in place of the ones it had.

You've finished work for the day and want your Home tabs in the same window.

<Screenshot name="home-switcher-crop" frame="crop-tl" alt="The Workspace switcher open over the Workspace panel, listing Work, Ceramics Shop, and Claude" />

## Steps

1. Above your tabs, select the current Workspace's name.
2. In the list, select the Workspace you want.

The window shows that Workspace's tabs and groups, and takes the
Workspace's color. You land on the tab you last looked at in that
Workspace on this device.

## Details

- The list shows every Workspace in the same order in every window. The
  Workspace the window is showing is in bold.
- If another window already shows a Workspace, the Workspace stays in that
  window. When you choose the Workspace, Sval brings the other window to
  the front. Your window stays where it is.
- The tabs of the Workspace you left stay in that Workspace, so they're
  there when you switch back.
- On the keyboard, use the arrow keys to reach a Workspace in the list, then
  press Enter.

- To go to the Workspace before or after the current one, in the list's
  order, swipe sideways with two fingers over the Workspace panel. With a
  mouse, scroll sideways, or hold Shift and turn the main wheel. Each swipe
  moves the window by one Workspace. A swipe skips device Workspaces and Agent
  Workspaces. If
  another window already shows the Workspace you swipe to, Sval brings that
  window to the front. Past your last Workspace, a swipe goes to your first,
  and before the first it goes to the last. You can turn swiping off in
  Settings › Keyboard shortcuts.

- In the menu bar, the **Workspaces** menu lists your Workspaces too.
