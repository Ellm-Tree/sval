An isolated Workspace keeps its own cookies and sign-ins. Sites in an
isolated Workspace can't see that you're signed in from your other
Workspaces.

You use Fastmail for work and for home. You isolate your Work Workspace, sign
in to your work account there, and stay signed in to your home account
everywhere else.

## Isolate a new Workspace

1. Above your tabs, select the current Workspace's name.
2. At the bottom of the list, choose **New Workspace…**.
3. In **Name**, type a name for the Workspace.
4. Check **Isolate this Workspace**.
5. Select **Create**.

## Isolate a Workspace you already have

1. Above your tabs, right-click the Workspace's name.
2. Choose **Isolate Workspace…**.
3. In the dialog, select **Isolate Workspace**.

Every tab in the Workspace reloads. The Workspace starts with no sign-ins, so
sign in again on the sites you use there. **Isolated** shows next to the
Workspace's name.

To isolate a Workspace the window isn't showing, open the Workspaces list and
right-click that Workspace there.

## Stop isolating a Workspace

1. Above your tabs, right-click the Workspace's name.
2. Choose **Stop isolating Workspace…**.
3. In the dialog, select **Stop isolating**.

Every tab in the Workspace reloads with the sign-ins of your other
Workspaces. Sval deletes the sign-ins and site data that were kept only in
the isolated Workspace.

## Details

- Whether a Workspace is isolated syncs to your other devices. The
  sign-ins don't, so you sign in once on each device. When you isolate a
  Workspace, your other devices reload the Workspace's tabs without
  asking.
- In the Workspaces list, an isolated Workspace has a box next to its
  name.
- When you move a tab into or out of an isolated Workspace, Sval asks
  first, then reloads the tab.
- Deleting an isolated Workspace deletes its sign-ins and site data too.
