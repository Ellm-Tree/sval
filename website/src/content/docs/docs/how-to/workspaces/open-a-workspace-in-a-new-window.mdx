Each window shows one Workspace. You can open another Workspace in a new
window and keep both on screen.

<Screenshot name="features-two-windows" alt="Two Sval windows side by side: the Work Workspace in blue and the Home Workspace in teal" />

You want Work open on one screen and Home on the other, without the two
mixing.

## Steps

1. Above your tabs, select the current Workspace's name.
2. In the list, point to the Workspace you want, then choose **Open in New Window**.

A new window opens on that Workspace, showing the tab you last looked at
there.

## Details

- To open a Workspace in a new window in one step, Shift-click the
  Workspace in the list. On the keyboard, press <kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd>.
- A Workspace shows in one window at a time. If another window already
  shows a Workspace, the list doesn't offer **Open in New Window** for it.
  Choosing that Workspace brings the other window to the front.
- When you move a tab from one window to the other, the tab joins that
  window's Workspace. See
  [Move a tab to another Workspace](/docs/how-to/workspaces/move-a-tab-to-another-workspace/).
