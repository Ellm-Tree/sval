When Archive idle tabs is on, Sval moves tabs you haven't used for a set
time out of their Workspace and into History, under Archived tabs. You can
get any archived tab back from there.

Your Work Workspace fills up with tabs you opened once and never went back
to. You want the old ones gone without closing each one yourself.

<Screenshot name="docs-idle-tabs" frame="rounded" maxWidth={600} alt="Settings › Workspaces › Idle tabs, with Archive idle tabs unchecked, After set to 7 Days, and Keep archived tabs for set to 30 days" />

## Turn it on for every Workspace

1. Open Settings › Workspaces.
2. Under Idle tabs, check **Archive idle tabs**.
3. In **After**, choose how long a tab can go unused.

## Choose a different time for one Workspace

1. Above your tabs, right-click the Workspace's name.
2. Choose **Edit…**.
3. In **Archive idle tabs**, choose a time, or **Never**.
4. Select **Save**.

Each Workspace starts on Use Default, which follows your choice in
Settings. If you choose something other than Use Default for one Workspace, that
choice applies even when Archive idle tabs is off in Settings.

## Details

- Archive idle tabs is off until you turn it on. When you turn it on, Sval
  picks 7 Days. The other times are 12 Hours, 24 Hours, and 30 Days.
- A tab counts as used when you stay on the tab for a few seconds after
  going to it, clicking in the page, or typing there. A use on any of your
  devices counts.
- Sval never archives these tabs:
  - A tab in a group.
  - A tab you're looking at in any window.
  - A tab that's playing sound.
  - A tab kept awake with Keep Awake.
  - A tab in picture-in-picture or a call.
  - A tab holding text you typed.
- Sval archives a Split only when every tab in the Split is idle. The Split
  comes back whole.
- **Keep archived tabs for.** This setting decides how long History keeps
  an archived tab: 7, 30, or 90 days. The setting starts at 30 days.
- Both settings sync to your other devices.
- Tabs in a private window are never archived.
