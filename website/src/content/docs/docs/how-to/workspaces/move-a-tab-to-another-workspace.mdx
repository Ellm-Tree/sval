A tab belongs to one Workspace. You can move a tab, or a whole group, to
another Workspace.

You opened a Codeberg issue while in Home, but the issue belongs with your
Work tabs.

<Screenshot name="docs-move-to-workspace" frame="rounded" maxWidth={400} alt="A tab's menu with Move to Workspace open, listing Home, Ceramics Shop, New Workspace…, and two agent Workspaces" />

## Move it with the menu

1. In the Workspace, right-click the tab, then choose **Move to Workspace**.
2. Choose the Workspace you want the tab in.

The tab leaves this Workspace and goes to the end of the other
Workspace. The window stays on the Workspace it was showing.

To move the tab to a Workspace that doesn't exist yet, choose **New Workspace…**
in step 2. Sval asks for the new Workspace's name, color, and emoji, then
moves the tab there.

## Drag it to another window

When the other Workspace is open in its own window:

1. Drag the tab's row into the other window's Workspace panel.
2. Drop the tab where you want it among that Workspace's tabs.

The tab joins that window's Workspace where you drop it.

## Details

- You can move a group the same way, with everything inside it.
  Right-click the group, then choose **Move to Workspace**.
- To move several tabs at once, select their rows first. To add a row to
  the selection, <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click the row (Ctrl-click on Windows and
  Linux). To select every row in between, Shift-click. Then right-click one
  of the selected rows.
- Dragging into another window moves one tab at a time. It doesn't move a
  group, a Split, or a selection of several rows.
- A tab you move to another window in any other way, for example with an
  extension, also joins that window's Workspace.
- When either Workspace is isolated, Sval asks before it moves the tab. The
  tab reloads in the other Workspace, and you might need to sign in again
  there. See [Isolate a Workspace](/docs/how-to/workspaces/isolate-a-workspace/).
