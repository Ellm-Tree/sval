Deleting a Workspace closes its tabs and removes its groups.

A project is over, and you don't need its Workspace or its tabs.

<Screenshot name="docs-workspace-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={200} alt="The menu for the Work Workspace: Edit…, Isolate Workspace…, and Delete Workspace…" />

## Steps

1. Above your tabs, right-click the Workspace's name.
2. Choose **Delete Workspace…**.
3. In the dialog, select **Delete Workspace**.

The window goes to another Workspace. For 8 seconds, **Undo** shows at the
bottom of the Workspace panel. Select **Undo** to put the Workspace back with
everything in it. You can also press <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> on
Windows and Linux).

## Details

- The dialog says how many tabs are inside the Workspace. Deleting a
  Workspace can't keep its tabs. To keep some of the tabs, move them to
  another Workspace first.
- You can't delete your last Workspace. **Delete Workspace…** is dimmed
  when you have only one.
- To delete a Workspace the window isn't showing, open the Workspaces list
  and right-click that Workspace there.
- When you delete a Workspace, Sval deletes the Workspace on your other
  devices too.
- Deleting an isolated Workspace also deletes its sign-ins and site data.
