---
title: Forget a site
description: Clear everything Sval keeps for a site, in every Workspace, from choices and history to cookies and caches.
---

When you forget a site, Sval clears everything it keeps for that site, in
every Workspace.

You stopped using a service and want no trace of it left in Sval.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Sites.
2. In Your sites, select the site's row.
3. At the bottom of the site's page, under Forget this site, select
   **Forget…**.
4. Check the list of what Sval clears.
5. Select the button that names the site, such as Forget example.com.

## Details

- The list shows how much the site holds in each part. The parts are
  settings for the site, fingerprinting exceptions, removed-element rules,
  Favorites and pins, history, downloads, cookies and site data, caches,
  permissions, and certificate exceptions.
- Parts listed under On every device are removed from your other devices
  too. Parts listed under On this device are cleared only on this device.
- You can't undo Forget.
- To forget a site that isn't listed, select **Add site** in Your sites.
  Type the site, then select **Add**.
- Forget isn't offered in a private window.

## If something goes wrong

- **One part of example.com wasn't forgotten.** Sval couldn't write to the
  disk. Select **Try again**.
