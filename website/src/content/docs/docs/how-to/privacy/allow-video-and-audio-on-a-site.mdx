Out of the box, every site is on Ask first. Nothing plays, with sound or
without, until you allow it. When a page tries to play, Sval asks whether
it can. You can also allow a site for one visit with the media button in the
address bar, and save a choice for the site in the site settings menu.

You open a Roon support video and it doesn't start. Later, you want a music
site you trust to always play.

<Screenshot name="home-site-controls-v2" alt="Site settings for bonappetit.com: zoom, element removal, Colorshift, video and audio, JavaScript, and per-site permissions" />

## Allow it for this visit

When the page tries to play, Sval asks under the media button. Select
**Allow for this visit**.

If you closed the question:

1. Look for the media button next to the address. The button lights up when
   the page has tried to play.
2. Select the media button.

Either way, the tab reloads, and the page can play video and audio. To stop
them again for the rest of the visit, select the media button.

## Choose what a site always gets

When Sval asks, select **Always allow**. The tab reloads, and the page plays.

To choose any time:

1. In the address bar, select the **Site settings** button.
2. Under Video and audio, choose **Always allow**, **Ask first**, or
   **Always deny**.
3. Close the menu.

When you choose Always allow, the tab reloads as the menu closes, so the
page can play.

## Change the default for every site

1. Open Settings › Sites.
2. Under Defaults, in **Video and audio**, choose **Always allow**,
   **Ask first**, or **Always deny**.

## Details

- Sval asks once a visit. If you select **Don't allow for this visit**, or
  close the question, Sval doesn't ask again until you leave the site.
- On Ask first, clicking elsewhere on the page doesn't allow video and
  audio.
- The media button's tooltip tells you what the button will do, such as
  "Click to allow for this visit".
- While a page shares your screen or uses your camera or microphone, you
  can't stop the page's video and audio.
- Your choice for a site syncs to your other devices, and so does the
  default.
- On an address like `nas.example.com`, use the menu's title to pick whether
  your choice covers that address alone or all of `example.com`.
