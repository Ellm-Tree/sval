---
title: Let a site use your camera or microphone
description: Answer a site's request for your camera or microphone, and mute them during a call.
---

When a site wants your camera or microphone, Sval asks you first. While the
site uses your camera or microphone, a button shows in the address bar. You
can mute the camera or microphone from that button.

You join a video call on a site you use every week and don't want to be
asked each time.

## Steps

1. When the site asks, choose **Allow**, **Always allow**, or **Block**.

Allow gives the site access for now. Always allow saves the permission for
the site. Block refuses the request.

## Mute your camera or microphone

1. In the address bar, select the camera or microphone button.

The button shows that the device is muted. The site gets black frames or
silence. To unmute, select the button again. The site doesn't ask again.

## Details

- **Always allow** shows only on a secure page, outside private windows.
- In an agent's tab, Sval asks you even on a site you always allow, and the
  question has no **Always allow**.
- The site settings menu lists what's in use under In use now. Each device
  has **Mute** or **Unmute**, and a shared screen has **Stop**.
- To change a saved permission, open the site settings menu. Under
  Permissions, choose a new answer next to Use the camera or Use the
  microphone. To clear the permission, select the ×. The tab reloads when
  you close the menu.
- To block every site from asking, open Settings › Sites. Under Defaults,
  set Camera or Microphone to **Block**.
