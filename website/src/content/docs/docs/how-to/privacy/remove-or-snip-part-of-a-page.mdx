Remove from this site saves a rule, so the part you removed stays hidden
every time you visit the site. Snip removes part of a page until you reload.

<Screenshot name="features-remove-element" alt="Remove from booking.com: a Google sign-in popup is highlighted, with a slider to widen or narrow what gets removed" />

A forum you read shows the same sign-up banner on every page. A news site
covers an article with a box you want gone for this visit only.

## Remove something on every visit

1. In the address bar, select the **Site settings** button.
2. Under Elements, choose **Remove from this site**….
3. On the page, select what you want to remove.
4. Optional: drag the slider toward Wider to remove more of what's around it.
5. Select **Remove**.

On an address like `nas.example.com`, the button says where Sval removes
the element, such as Remove from all of example.com.

A message tells you where the element was removed, and offers **Undo**.

## Snip something until you reload

1. In the address bar, select the scissors button.
2. On the page, select what you want to snip.

The message **Snipped. Reload brings it back.** shows, with **Undo**.

## Details

- At first, the scissors button is pinned to the address bar. The eraser
  button starts Remove from this site. To pin the eraser too, select the pin
  next to Remove from this site in the site settings menu.
- On the keyboard, Tab moves between elements, Enter chooses one, and
  Escape leaves.
- The Remove from this site card counts how many elements the rule removes
  on the page. When you remove an image, a frame, a video, or audio,
  **Stop the download too** keeps it from loading at all.
- On an address like `nas.example.com`, use the card's title to pick
  whether to remove the element on that address or on all of `example.com`.
  The title starts on the address.
- Sval saves your removals in My filters, and they sync to your other
  devices. Removed elements stay removed even when you turn off ad blocking
  or a site's protections.
- A number on the eraser or the scissors shows how much is removed or
  snipped on the page. When the button isn't pinned, the number shows on the
  Site settings button.
- To bring something back, open the site settings menu, then open Removed
  from this site under Elements. Select **Show again**. On an address like
  `nas.example.com`, you choose whether to show the element on all of the
  site or on that address only.
- Remove from this site is off in private windows, because it saves a rule.
  Snip works in private windows.

## If something goes wrong

- **Couldn't save. My filters is full.** Remove rules you no longer need
  from My filters, in Settings › Privacy and protection, then try again.
