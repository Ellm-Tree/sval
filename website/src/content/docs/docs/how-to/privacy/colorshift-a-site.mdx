Colorshift changes how a site looks for you. Sval shows your changes every
time you visit the site, and syncs them to your other devices.

A site you read every evening has a bright white page. Another uses a thin,
small font that's hard to read.

## Open Colorshift

1. In the address bar, select the **Site settings** button.
2. Under Colorshift, choose **Colorshift This Site**….

You can also choose **Colorshift This Site**… when you right-click the page
or a tab in the Workspace, or choose Colorshift this site in the Launcher.
To open Colorshift with a key, give Colorshift this site a key in
Settings › Keyboard shortcuts.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

To keep a Colorshift button in the address bar, open the site settings
menu and select the pin on the Colorshift row. The button opens
Colorshift, and it stays lit while Colorshift is open. To take the
button away, select the pin again.

</Since>

Colorshift opens over the page. The page shows each
change as you make it, and nothing is kept until you select **Save**.

## Tint the page

1. Drag the big dot on the color wheel toward the color you want. The
   farther the dot is from the middle, the stronger the tint.
2. Optional: drag the small dot around the wheel to pick the color for the
   page's text and other dark colors.

The page shows the tint as you drag. Light colors, like the page's
background, take the big dot's color. Dark colors, like the page's text,
take the small dot's color. To take the tint away, drag the big dot back to
the middle.

## Change the contrast, brightness, or saturation

Drag the **Contrast**, **Brightness**, or **Saturation** slider. The middle
of each slider leaves the page as it is.

## Pick a light or dark theme

Under Theme, choose **Automatic**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

- **Automatic** lets the site follow Sval's own light or dark theme.
- **Dark** asks the site for its own dark theme. When the site has none,
  Sval turns the page's colors dark and leaves pictures and videos as they
  are.
- **Light** asks the site for its own light theme. When the site has none,
  Sval turns a dark page's colors light.

## Change the font

Select the menu next to **Font**, then pick one.

- **Original** keeps the site's own font.
- **System** is your computer's interface font.
- **Serif** and **Monospace** use your computer's serif and monospace fonts.
- **Readable** is Atkinson Hyperlegible when you've installed it, and
  Verdana or DejaVu Sans otherwise.

## Keep your changes or go back

- To keep your changes, select **Save**. Sval shows the site that way every
  time you visit, and Colorshift closes.
- To go back, select **Cancel**. The page looks the way it did when you
  opened Colorshift, and Colorshift closes. **Close** and Escape do the
  same.

<Screenshot name="docs-colorshift-row" frame="rounded" maxWidth={360} alt="The site settings menu for inkandswitch.com with a saved Colorshift: a dot on the Site settings button and on the Colorshift row, which reads Orange tint" />

## Start over

When the site has a saved Colorshift, select **Reset style**. The page
shows the site's own look. Select **Save** to remove the saved Colorshift,
or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Details

- A tint moves each of the page's colors toward the color you picked. Each
  color moves toward the color you picked more than it gets lighter or
  darker, so text stays readable against its background.
- With the keyboard, select a dot and use the arrow keys. Left and Right
  turn the dot around the wheel. Up and Down move both dots out from the
  middle or back in.
- With Light or Dark chosen under Theme, the page keeps your tint and
  slider changes.
- Colorshift stays open while you use the page or the site settings menu. To move Colorshift, drag its title. Switching
  tabs closes Colorshift as **Cancel** does.
- On an address like `nas.example.com`, the site's name in the title is a
  menu. Use the menu to pick whether the changes cover that address or all
  of `example.com`. The menu starts on that address.
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- When the site has a saved Colorshift, a dot shows on the Site settings
  button, unless the button shows a count. The Colorshift row in the site
  settings menu carries the same dot and lists what the Colorshift changes,
  such as "Blue tint, dark theme, serif font".

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- When the site has a saved Colorshift, a dot shows on the Colorshift
  button if you pinned it. Otherwise the dot shows on the Site settings
  button, unless that button shows a count. The Colorshift row in the
  site settings menu carries the same dot and lists what the Colorshift
  changes, such as "Blue tint, dark theme, serif font".

</Since>

- Colorshift is off in private windows, because it saves your changes.
- Forget this site removes the site's Colorshift changes.
- The color wheel comes from Zen Browser's Boosts.
- Colorshift changes colors and fonts. To hide part of a page, see
  [Remove or snip part of a page](/docs/how-to/privacy/remove-or-snip-part-of-a-page/).

## If something goes wrong

- **With Dark chosen, a dark page shows light for a moment.** Sval turns the
  page dark as it loads. Once Sval sees the page is already dark, it stops
  changing the page's colors.
- **Icons turned into letters or boxes.** The site draws its icons with a
  font. Choose **Original** under Font.
