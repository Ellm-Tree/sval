---
title: Stop Sval blocking a domain
description: Add a domain Sval never blocks, at any level and on every site.
---

Sval never blocks a domain you allow, on any site and at any level.

A list blocks the domain your bank's site loads its sign-in page from, and
you can't sign in.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Privacy and protection.
2. Under Allowed domains, select **Add domain**.
3. In **Domain**, type the domain or paste an address.
4. Select **Add**.

Sval never blocks requests to that domain or any address under it.

## Details

- Allowing `cdn.example.com` also allows every address under it, such as
  `img.cdn.example.com`.
- To remove an allowed domain, select the × at the end of its row.
- Allowed domains sync to your other devices.
- To stop blocking on one site instead, turn off that site's protections.
  See [Turn off protections for a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/turn-off-protections-for-a-site/).
