Sval blocks ads and trackers with filter lists. The level you choose decides
which lists Sval uses.

A news site you read keeps loading trackers from other companies, and you
want Sval to block more of them.

<Screenshot name="docs-blocking-levels" alt="Settings › Privacy and protection: Ad and tracker blocking set to Balanced Blocking, with the Ads and Trackers lists in use" />

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Privacy and protection.
2. Under Ad and tracker blocking, choose a **Level**.

The line under the levels describes the level you chose. The change takes
effect the next time a page loads.

## Details

- **Balanced Blocking.** A new install starts on this level. It blocks ads
  and trackers with lists that rarely break sites.

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Strict Blocking.** This level adds lists that block more, including
  trackers in links, social widgets, and scam sites. Some sites might break.
  Strict Blocking also strips tracking parameters from the addresses you
  open.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Strict Blocking.** This level adds lists that block more, including
  trackers in links, social widgets, and scam sites. Some sites might break.

</Since>

- **No Blocking.** Sval blocks nothing from the lists.
- **Custom.** You start from the lists of the level you were on. Then you
  can switch lists on or off, and add your own. See
  [Add a filter list](/docs/how-to/privacy/add-a-filter-list/).
- On any level other than Custom, the card shows the lists in use and who
  publishes each one. Sval downloads each list from its publisher and keeps
  the list updated.
- Sval checks a list for an update when the list's publisher says the copy
  is out of date. Sval waits at least 12 hours between checks of a list,
  and never more than about 15 days. Sval checks only while it's open. If
  a check fell due while Sval was closed, Sval checks a few minutes after
  you open it.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- On every level except No Blocking, Sval strips tracking parameters from
  the addresses you open. A tracking parameter is the part of an address
  that tells a site which ad, email, or post you came from. Sval takes the
  parameters from Mozilla's list and from the parameters the lists in use
  strip on every site. The parameters change when those lists update.

</Since>

- Your level syncs to your other devices.
- When you leave Custom for another level, Sval asks first, because the new
  level's lists replace your custom set. Sval removes the lists you added.
- To stop blocking on one site only, turn off that site's protections. See
  [Turn off protections for a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/turn-off-protections-for-a-site/).

## If something goes wrong

- **Couldn't load this list today.** The list's publisher didn't answer.
  Sval keeps using the copy it last downloaded.
- **Couldn't load this list. Nothing from it is blocked yet.** Sval has never
  downloaded the list. Sval tries again by itself.
- **A site breaks after you choose Strict Blocking.** Turn off protections
  for that site, or go back to **Balanced Blocking**.
