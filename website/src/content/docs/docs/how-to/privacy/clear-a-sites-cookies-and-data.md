---
title: Clear a site's cookies and data
description: Sign out of a site by clearing its cookies and site data, in this tab's Workspace or in every Workspace.
---

Clearing a site's cookies and site data signs you out of the site.

A site keeps showing you an old version of your account, and signing out
doesn't help.

## Clear it where you are

1. In the address bar, select the **Site settings** button.
2. Select **Clear cookies and site data**.
3. Select **Clear and reload**.

The tab reloads, and you're signed out of the site.

## Clear it in every Workspace

1. Open Settings › Sites.
2. In Your sites, select the site's row.
3. Under Cookies and site data, select **Clear everywhere**.
4. Select **Clear and sign out**.

## Details

- Clearing from the site settings menu removes the site's data only from
  the place where this tab keeps it. An isolated Workspace keeps its own
  cookies, and so does a private window. Clearing one of them leaves the others as they
  are.
- On the site's page, Cookies and site data lists each place that holds
  data for the site, such as Shared Workspaces or an isolated Workspace.
  Each place has its own **Clear** button.
- Clearing doesn't change the site's protections or your other choices for
  the site. To clear those too, forget the site. See
  [Forget a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/forget-a-site/).

## If something goes wrong

- **Not cleared. Sval couldn't write to the disk.** Select **Try again** on
  that row.
