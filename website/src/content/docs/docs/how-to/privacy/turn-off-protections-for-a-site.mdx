To turn off Sval's protections for the site you're on, use the Privacy and
Security button. The button sits in the address bar, next to the site's
address.

A shop's checkout won't load, and you think blocking is the cause.

<Screenshot name="home-privacy" frame="rounded" maxWidth={520} alt="The Privacy and Security panel for cnn.com, showing the trackers and ads blocked and the site's protection switches" />

## Turn off all protections

1. In the address bar, select the Privacy and Security button.
2. Under This site, turn off **Protections for this site**.

The tab reloads. The Privacy and Security panel's header then reads
**Protections off for this site**. The Privacy and Security panel also
counts the trackers the page loaded.

## Turn off fingerprinting protection only

1. In the address bar, select the Privacy and Security button.
2. Under This site, turn off **Fingerprinting protection**.

The tab reloads. Sval still blocks trackers and ads on the site. The site
can now read your device's real details.

## Details

- To turn protections back on, turn the same switch on again.
- Protections block trackers and ads, and they resist fingerprinting. When
  protections are off, **Fingerprinting protection** is off too, and you
  can't change it.
- Parts of pages you removed with Remove from this site stay removed when
  protections are off.
- On an address like `nas.example.com`, use the
  Privacy and Security panel's title to pick whether your choice covers
  that address alone or all of `example.com`. See
  [Make a choice for one address on a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/make-a-choice-for-one-address/).
- Your choices sync to your other devices. To see the site in Settings ›
  Sites, select **Manage sites** at the bottom of the
  Privacy and Security panel.
- In a private window, your choice lasts until you close the window, and it
  never syncs. In a private window, you can't turn off fingerprinting
  protection by itself. Turn off all protections for the site instead.
- To let a site read only some device details, give the site a
  fingerprinting exception. See
  [Give a site a fingerprinting exception](/docs/how-to/privacy/give-a-site-a-fingerprinting-exception/).
