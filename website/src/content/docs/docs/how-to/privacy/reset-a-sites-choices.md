---
title: Reset a site's choices
description: Put a site back on the defaults, while keeping your sign-ins and site data.
---

Resetting a site puts its protections, video and audio, JavaScript,
fingerprinting exceptions, permissions, and extension switches back on the
defaults. Your cookies and sign-ins stay.

You turned off a site's protections months ago and want to start again from
Sval's defaults.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Sites.
2. In Your sites, find the site. To narrow the list, use **Find a site**.
3. On the site's row, select **Reset**.

A message shows **Reset** and the site's name, with **Undo**.

## Details

- If you removed parts of the site's pages, Sval asks before it resets the
  site, because Reset deletes those rules from My filters. You can't undo
  that Reset.
- When you reset an address listed under its site, Sval removes only that
  address's choices. The address then follows the site for everything.
- Resetting a site leaves the choices you made for addresses under it.
- Resetting syncs to your other devices.
- To also clear the site's cookies, history, and everything else Sval
  keeps, forget the site. See [Forget a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/forget-a-site/).
