---
title: Turn on Mozilla's compatibility fixes
description: Use the list Mozilla publishes that weakens fingerprinting protection on Google's and Bing's sites.
---

Mozilla publishes a list that weakens fingerprinting protection on Google's
and Bing's sites. Sval doesn't use the list unless you turn it on, so
Google's and Bing's sites get full protection.

A Google service isn't working right for you, and you want to try it with
Mozilla's fixes.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Privacy and protection.
2. Under Fingerprinting, open **Advanced**.
3. Select **Compatibility**.
4. Turn on **Mozilla's compatibility fixes**.

## Details

- The fixes might help those sites work. The fixes also make it easier for
  those sites to recognize your device.
- With the fixes on, open the Privacy and Security button on a site they
  cover. **Fingerprinting protection** shows as off, with a note that
  Mozilla's fix weakens protection there. To keep full protection on that
  site, turn the switch on. That choice syncs to your other devices.
- While the fixes are on, private windows always use them. In a private
  window, the switch can't turn full protection back on.
