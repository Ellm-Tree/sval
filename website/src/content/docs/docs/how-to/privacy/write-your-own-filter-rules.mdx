My filters holds your own rules, one per line, in uBlock Origin's syntax.
Sval uses your rules along with the lists.

You want to block one ad server that no list catches, or hide a banner on a
site you use every day.

## Steps

1. Open Settings › Privacy and protection.
2. Under Ad and tracker blocking, open **Advanced**.
3. In **My filters**, type one rule per line.
4. Select **Save**.

**Saved.** shows under the box.

<Screenshot name="docs-my-filters" alt="Settings › Privacy and protection › Advanced, with three rules in My filters and the Save button" />

## Details

- A rule like `||ads.example.com^` blocks an address. A rule like
  `example.com##.banner` hides part of a page.
- Parts of pages you remove with Remove from this site are saved here too.
  See [Remove or snip part of a page](/docs/how-to/privacy/remove-or-snip-part-of-a-page/).
- Your rules sync to your other devices.
- On **No Blocking**, your rules have no effect, apart from the parts of
  pages you removed.
- **Let My filters use trusted scriptlets.** Your rules can then run
  trusted scriptlets. Sval asks first. The trust covers your rules as you
  saved them on this device. When your rules change on another device, you
  trust them again on this one.

## If something goes wrong

- **These filters are too long to save.** Remove some rules, then select
  **Save** again.
- **Filters can only hold plain text.** Remove anything that isn't text.
