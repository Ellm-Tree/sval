---
title: Share your location with a site
description: Answer a site's request for your location, and block or allow it later from the location button.
---

When a site asks for your location, a location button shows next to the
site controls. You answer from there.

A map site asks where you are so it can show nearby shops.

## Steps

1. When the site asks, select the location button next to the site
   controls, if the prompt isn't already open.
2. Choose **Allow** or **Block**.

## Change your answer later

1. In the address bar, select the location button.

While the site uses your location, the button is lit. Selecting the lit
button blocks location on that site. While location is blocked, the button
is crossed out. Selecting the crossed-out button allows location on that
site. Either way, the page reloads.

## Details

- The location button shows while a site asks for your location, and while
  the site uses your location. A crossed-out button stays until you leave
  the page.
- Sval gets your location from your operating system alone.
- In an agent's tab, Sval asks you even on a site where you allowed
  location, and doesn't offer to remember your answer.
- To block every site from asking, open Settings › Sites. Under Defaults,
  set Location to **Block**.
