---
title: Search Kagi from private tabs
description: Sign in to Kagi in a normal tab, and private tabs search Kagi with Privacy Pass.
since: ios-0.1.0
---

Private tabs on your iPhone or iPad can search Kagi without telling Kagi
who you are. Normal tabs use your Kagi sign-in, and private tabs use
Privacy Pass, which shows Kagi you're a subscriber without saying which
one.

You search with Kagi on your computer, and you want private searches on
your iPhone to work without linking them to your account.

## Steps

1. On your computer, choose Kagi as your search engine. See
   [Change your search engine](/docs/how-to/search/change-your-search-engine/).
   Your iPhone or iPad takes the same engine once it syncs.
2. On your iPhone or iPad, open a normal tab and go to kagi.com.
3. Sign in to your Kagi account.

From then on, a search in a private tab reaches Kagi signed in with
Privacy Pass.

## Details

- Your Kagi sign-in and your Privacy Pass searches stay on the device and
  never sync, so each iPhone, iPad, and computer keeps its own.
- Each private search uses up one Privacy Pass search. When you've used
  this month's, a private search shows This month's private Kagi searches
  are used up, with the date they reset. Normal tabs still search Kagi as
  usual.
- If your Kagi plan doesn't include Privacy Pass, private tabs reach Kagi
  signed out.
