Sval can hide a cookie notice or a banner on a site for good. You tap the
part of the page you want gone, and Sval saves a rule that hides it on
every visit and on every device you sync.

A news site covers half the screen with a newsletter banner each time you
open it.

## Steps

1. In the bar, select the shield next to the address. The site menu opens.
2. Select **Remove from This Site**.
3. Tap the part of the page you want gone.
4. To take in more of the page around it, drag the slider toward the
   larger square.
5. Select **Remove**.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-remove" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The Remove panel over a Yosemite page, with a slider between a small and a large square, and the Remove button" />

The part you picked disappears, and Undo shows for a moment.

## Details

- On an address like news.example.com, the menu next to **Remove** chooses
  between that address alone and all of example.com.
- The site menu lists the site's rules under Removed from This Site. To
  bring back what a rule hides, select the rule's ×.
- **Remove from This Site** is off in private tabs, because it saves a
  rule that syncs.
