As you type in the Launcher, Sval finds your matching tabs
and history and lists the searches your search engine suggests.

You want the Postgres docs page you read on your computer yesterday, and
you remember only the word "vacuum".

## Steps

1. In the bar at the bottom of the screen, select the address. To start
   in a new tab, select **New Tab** instead.
2. Type a word from the page's title or address.
3. Select a row from the list above the field.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-launcher" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The Launcher with yosemite typed, listing search suggestions, History, and a row that searches for yosemite" />

## What the list shows

The row nearest the field searches for what you typed, or opens the
address you typed. Above that row, the Launcher groups what it found:

- **Open tabs.** Select **Switch to Tab** to go to the tab.
- **Pins and Favorites** and **History.** Select a row to open the page.
- **Search suggestions.** Your search engine suggests searches for what
  you typed. Select one to search for it.

## Details

- The Launcher searches with the search engine you chose on your computer,
  and follows the Launcher settings you chose there, from the sections it
  shows to their order.
- Before you type, the Launcher lists tabs you closed recently, your recent
  tabs, and your pins.
- A private tab gets no search suggestions, and the Launcher lists only
  private tabs under Private tabs.
- Sval sends nothing to your search engine for a single character or
  for what looks like an address.
- Type "private" to find **New Private Tab**.
