When your iPhone or iPad leaves the sync folder, it stops syncing, and your
other devices keep syncing through the folder as before. You choose whether
the device keeps a copy of your Workspaces.

You're giving your old iPhone to someone, and you want Sval on it to stop
syncing and forget your Workspaces.

## Steps

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. At the bottom of your Workspace, select **Settings**.
3. Under Sync, select **Leave This Folder**.
4. Select **Keep Workspaces on This iPhone**, or **Start Over**. On an
   iPad, the first button reads **Keep Workspaces on This iPad**.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-settings-sync" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The Sync section of Settings, with Synced, the iCloud Drive folder, Sync with Another Device, and Leave This Folder" />

## Details

- **Keep Workspaces on This iPhone** keeps your Workspaces and tabs on the
  device. Changes you make there no longer reach your other devices.
- **Start Over** also removes your Workspaces and your sync key from the
  device, and setup opens as it did when you first installed Sval.
- To sync again, select **Set Up Sync…** in Settings and choose the folder
  your other devices use.
