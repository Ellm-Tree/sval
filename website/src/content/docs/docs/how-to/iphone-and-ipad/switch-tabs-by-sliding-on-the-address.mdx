You can go from tab to tab without opening your Workspace.
Hold the address and slide, and the Workspace's pages pass under your
finger.

You're reading a recipe and want the shopping list two tabs over, then the
recipe again.

## Steps

1. In the bar at the bottom of the screen, touch and hold the address.
   A strip of the Workspace's pages rises over the bar, with the page in
   front at its center.
2. Without lifting your finger, slide left or right. Each page moves to
   the front as your finger passes it.
3. Lift your finger on the page you want.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-tab-scrub" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The strip of the Utah group's pages above the bar, with the Zion page at the center" />

## Details

- The strip lists your pins first, under the heading Pinned, then the
  Workspace's tabs in the order the Workspace shows them.
- A Split shows in the strip as one card of its pages. Lift on the card to
  open the Split.
- When a page in the strip is in a group, the strip takes the group's name
  and color.
- To move through many tabs, rest your finger near the edge of the screen.
  The strip runs on toward that end until you move your finger back.
- A tab whose page Sval can't open on your iPhone or iPad, such as a
  Settings page from your computer, stays out of the strip.
- To stay on the page you started from, slide back to it and lift there.
- A tap on the address opens the Launcher instead.
