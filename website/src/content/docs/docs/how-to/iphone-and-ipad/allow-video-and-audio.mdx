Sval on your iPhone or iPad plays a site's video and audio only once you
allow it, and every site starts on Ask first. Your choices for each site
sync, so a site you allow on your computer plays on your iPhone or iPad too.

A cooking video won't start, and you want it to play. Later, you want a
music site to always play.

## Allow it for this visit

When a page tries to play, **Allow Video and Audio** shows above the bar,
and selecting **Allow Video and Audio** reloads the page so the video can
play.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-media-ask" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="Allow Video and Audio above the bar, on a page with a video that hasn't started" />

## Choose what a site always gets

1. In the bar, select the shield next to the address. The site menu opens.
2. Next to Video and Audio, choose **Always allow**, **Ask first**, or
   **Always deny**.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-site-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The site menu for nps.gov, with Video and Audio set to Ask first" />

To save Always allow straight from the question above the bar, hold
**Allow Video and Audio** and choose **Always Allow on This Site**.

## Choose for every site

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. At the bottom of your Workspace, select **Settings**.
3. Under Video and Audio, choose what every site gets.

## Details

- Sites that have a choice of their own are listed in Settings, under
  Video and Audio › Sites. To give a site the same choice as every other
  site again, select **Use Default** next to it.
- A choice in a private tab lasts until you exit the private Workspace,
  and doesn't sync.
- Video and audio keep playing when you switch tabs or leave Sval, unless
  you turn off **Keep playing after you leave a tab** in Settings, under
  Video and Audio.
- To play a video in Picture in Picture, select the Video and Audio button
  in the bar, then the Picture in Picture button.
