Private tabs live in a private Workspace of their own. Sval keeps no
history from them and syncs nothing in them, and when you exit the private
Workspace, Sval deletes their cookies and site data.

You're shopping for a present on a shared iPad, and you don't want the
gift to show up in your history or on your other devices.

## Open a private tab

1. In the bar at the bottom of the screen, touch and hold the plus button,
   **New Tab**.
2. Choose **New Private Tab**.

The private Workspace opens, with the Launcher ready for an address. You
can also select the Private tile at the end of the Workspace strip.

## Go back to your Workspaces

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. Select **Back to**, followed by your Workspace's name.

Your private tabs stay open, and the Private tile in the strip shows how
many there are.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-private" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The private Workspace with no tabs, with Back to iPhone 17 Pro and Exit at the bottom" />

## Exit the private Workspace

1. With a private tab in front, select the Workspace button at the bottom
   right.
2. Select **Exit**.

Sval closes every private tab and deletes their cookies and site data.
You can also hold the Private tile and choose
**Exit Private Workspace**.

## Details

- The private Workspace stays open after you close its last tab, until
  you select **Exit**.
- Closing a private tab has no Undo.
- A link you select in a private tab opens in Sval, even when another app
  on your iPhone or iPad could open that link.
- The Launcher shows no search suggestions in a private tab.
- If Kagi is your search engine, private tabs search Kagi with Privacy
  Pass. See [Search Kagi from private tabs](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/search-kagi-from-private-tabs/).
- Video and audio stop when you leave a private tab or leave Sval.
- Files you download in a private tab stay in the Files app after you
  exit.
- Websites and your network can still see where you go.
