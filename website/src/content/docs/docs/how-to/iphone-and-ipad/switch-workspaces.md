---
title: Switch Workspaces
description: Go to another Workspace from the Workspace button, the bar, or the strip of Workspaces.
since: ios-0.1.0
---

Your iPhone or iPad shows one Workspace at a time, the same Workspaces you keep on
your other devices. You can switch with a slide on the Workspace button or a
swipe on the bar, or pick a Workspace from the strip in your Workspace.

You read the news in your Home Workspace on the train, then switch to Work
when you get to the office.

## Hold the Workspace button and slide

1. At the bottom right, touch and hold the Workspace button. A strip of
   your Workspaces rises over the bar.
2. Without lifting your finger, slide left or right to the Workspace you
   want.
3. Lift your finger.

## Swipe the bar

Swipe the bar at the bottom of the screen to the left for the next
Workspace, or to the right for the one before. A swipe past the last
Workspace goes around to the first.

## Pick from the strip

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. In the strip at the bottom of your Workspace, select the Workspace you
   want.

Your Workspace stays open on the one you picked, so you can choose a tab
there.

## Details

- The strip lists this device's own Workspace first, then your Workspaces
  in the order you keep them in. The Workspaces your other devices keep
  for themselves follow under Devices, and each agent's Workspaces under
  the agent's name.
- To put a Workspace somewhere else in the strip, hold its tile and drag
  it. You can also hold the tile and choose **Move Left** or
  **Move Right**.
- To make a Workspace, select **New** at the end of the strip.
- Swiping the bar and sliding on the Workspace button skip the private
  Workspace, which opens from the Private tile in the strip. See
  [Browse privately](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/browse-privately/).
