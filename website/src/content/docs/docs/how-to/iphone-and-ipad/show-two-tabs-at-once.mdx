A Split shows two tabs on screen at once. On an iPhone, the pages sit one
above the other while you hold the iPhone upright, and side by side when
you turn it sideways. On an iPad, the pages sit one above the other
whichever way you hold the iPad. A Split you make on your iPhone or iPad
syncs to your computer, and a Split you make on your computer shows as a
Split on your iPhone or iPad.

<Screenshot name="docs-ipad-split" frame="rounded" maxWidth={640} alt="An iPad held sideways, with the Swift page above the WebKit page in a Split" />

You're filling in a form on one page and copying details from an email on
another.

## Steps

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. In your Workspace, touch and hold the first tab.
3. Choose **Split With…**.
4. Select the second tab.

The two pages show together, and the bar at the bottom acts on the page
with the ring around it. Touch the other page to move the ring there.

## Change the Split

- To give one page more room, drag the divider between the pages.
- The Split button next to the address opens the Split's menu, where you
  can move the page with the ring up or down, rename the Split, or close
  its tabs.
- **Separate** in that menu puts the pages back as two tabs.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-split" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="A Split of the Swift and WebKit pages, with the Split's menu open over the WebKit page" />

## Details

- You can also make a Split by dragging one tab onto the middle of
  another. See [Rearrange tabs in the grid](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/rearrange-tabs-in-the-grid/).
- A Split holds up to four pages. With three or four, a tray in the
  divider holds a tile for each page, and the arrows in the tray bring the
  next page on screen.
- The divider's place applies to this device alone.
