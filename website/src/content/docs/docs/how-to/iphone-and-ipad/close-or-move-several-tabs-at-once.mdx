Edit Tabs lets you check several tabs in your Workspace and act on all of
them in one go.

You've finished a project, and the eight tabs you opened for it can close
together.

## Steps

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. At the top of your Workspace, select the Workspace options button,
   the three dots.
3. Select **Edit Tabs…**. In a Workspace with groups, the item reads
   **Edit Tabs and Groups…**.
4. Select each tab you want. A check mark shows on each one.
5. At the bottom of the screen, select **Close Selected**,
   **Move Selected…**, or **New Group…**.
6. Select **Done**.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-edit-tabs" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="Edit Tabs with three tabs checked, and Select All and Done at the top" />

## Details

- To check every tab, select **Select All** at the top left.
- **Move Selected…** moves the tabs to another Workspace or group.
- **New Group…** puts the tabs in a new group and asks for its name and
  color.
- After **Close Selected**, Undo shows for a moment.
- Edit Tabs checks single tabs. Open a closed group before you check a tab
  inside it, and hold a Split to move or close the Split from its menu.
