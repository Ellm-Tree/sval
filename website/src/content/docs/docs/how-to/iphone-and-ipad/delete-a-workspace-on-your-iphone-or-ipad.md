---
title: Delete a Workspace on your iPhone or iPad
description: Delete a Workspace and every tab and group in it, on all your devices.
since: ios-0.1.0
---

Deleting a Workspace closes its tabs and removes its groups on every
device you sync.

A trip is over, and you don't need its Workspace on your iPhone or your
computer.

## Steps

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. In the strip at the bottom of your Workspace, touch and hold the
   Workspace's tile.
3. Choose **Delete Workspace…**.
4. In the question that opens, select **Delete Workspace**.

Sval goes to the next Workspace in the strip.

## Details

- To keep some of the tabs, move them to another Workspace before you
  delete the Workspace.
- You can also delete the Workspace you're in. Select its name at the top
  of your Workspace, then **Delete This Workspace…**.
- You can't delete your last Workspace, or this device's own Workspace,
  where the tabs you open on this device go.
- Deleting a Workspace on your iPhone or iPad has no Undo.
