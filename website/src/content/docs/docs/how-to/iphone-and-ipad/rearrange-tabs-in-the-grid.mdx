In the grid, you can drag a tab's card to a new place. Drop the card on
another card, and the two tabs go into a group or a Split. Each card shows
a picture of its tab's page.

You've opened five tabs while planning a trip, and you want the flights and
the hotel together in one group.

## Show the tabs as a grid

1. At the bottom right, select the Workspace button.
2. At the top of your Workspace, select the Workspace options button,
   the three dots.
3. Under Show Tabs As, select **Grid**.

## Move a card

1. Touch and hold a card until it lifts.
2. Drag the card to where you want it, and pause. The other cards make
   room.
3. Lift your finger.

## Group two tabs or make a Split

1. Touch and hold a card until it lifts.
2. Drag the card over the middle of another card. That card shows two
   halves, Group and Split.
3. Lift your finger over the half you want.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-grid-drop" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="A Death Valley card held over another card in the grid, which shows a Group half and a Split half" />

Group asks for the new group's name and color. Split puts the two tabs on
screen at once.

## Details

- To close a tab, select × on its card, or swipe the card sideways. Undo
  shows at the top of the screen for a moment.
- A card playing video or audio shows a Stop button in the corner of its
  picture.
- The tab in front has a ring around its card.
- Your choice of grid or list applies to this device alone. You can also
  change it in Settings › Show Tabs As.
- To move a tab without dragging, hold its card and choose **Move to…**.
  To make a Split, hold the card and choose **Split With…**.
