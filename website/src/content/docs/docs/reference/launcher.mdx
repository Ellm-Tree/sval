The Launcher is the address bar, opened over the page. What you type in
the Launcher can go to a page, search the web, switch to an open tab in
any Workspace, or run one of Sval's commands. The search field on the home
page lists the same rows and answers the same keys.

<Screenshot name="features-launcher" alt="The Launcher searching lisbon, with open tabs from other Workspaces, a Favorite, and history" />

## The address bar

The address bar sits in the toolbar. While you're not typing, the address
bar shows the page's address the way you chose in Settings › Appearance › Address bar.

- The site name is shown without "www.". The rest of the address is
  dimmed.
- When the address doesn't fit, Sval drops whole parts from the left, so
  the end of the address stays in view.
- When there's room, the address bar shows the Launcher's shortcut at its
  right end.
- When you click the address bar, or reach it with <kbd>F6</kbd>, the list opens right
  there in the toolbar.

## Opening the Launcher

<kbd>⌘L</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+L</kbd> on Windows and Linux) opens the Launcher in the middle of the
window, over the page, with the address selected. <kbd>Alt+D</kbd> does the same on
Windows and Linux. In the menu bar, **File › Launcher…** opens it too.

- Escape, or a click outside the Launcher, closes the Launcher and takes
  you back to the page.
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- With nothing typed, the Launcher lists your most recently used tabs
  under Recent tabs.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- With nothing typed, the Launcher lists your most recently used tabs
  under Recent tabs. The sites you visit most follow under Frequent sites,
  except any you already have open in a tab.

</Since>
- The field reads "Search, go to a tab, or run a command" until you type.

## Sections

As you type, the Launcher lists what matches, in sections. The first row
is what Enter opens: a search for what you typed, or the site when you
type an address or Sval completes one. An open tab is never the first row.
When what you typed starts a word in an open tab's title, or starts the
tab's domain, Open tabs comes right after the first row. Otherwise the
sections come in this order until you change it:

- **Open tabs.** This section lists up to 4 open tabs from every
  Workspace. A tab in another Workspace shows that Workspace's emoji and
  name, in the Workspace's color. Enter on that tab switches to its
  Workspace.
- **Pins and Favorites.** This section lists up to 3 of your pins and
  Favorites.
- **Commands.** This section lists up to 3 of Sval's commands whose names
  match.
- **History.** This section lists up to 5 pages you've visited.
- **Search suggestions.** This section lists up to 3 suggestions from your
  search engine.

Each page shows once, in the first section it fits. Typing % and a
space lists every open tab.

## What Enter does

The selected row names what Enter does there: **Open**, **Search**,
**Switch to tab**, Switch to and a Workspace's name, **Go to pin**, **Go to
Favorite**, **Run**, or **Choose**.

The line under the list names the other keys that work on the selected
row. It also says what each key does there.

- **New tab:** <kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd> opens the page in a new tab.
- **New window:** <kbd>⇧⌘Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Enter</kbd>) opens the page in a new
  window.
- **Split:** <kbd>⌥Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd>) opens the page next to this tab in a
  Split. On an open tab, it reads **Split with this tab**.
- **Remove:** <kbd>⇧⌫</kbd> (<kbd>Shift+Delete</kbd> on Windows and
  Linux) removes a History or Search suggestions row, so the row stops
  showing.
- **Runs it from anywhere** names the command's own shortcut. **No
  shortcut** means the command has none.
- **Opens a list** marks a command that asks you to choose something next.
- **Close:** Escape.

On an open tab, every Enter key except <kbd>⌥Enter</kbd> switches to that tab. A
modifier never opens a second copy of the tab. A Split never mixes an
agent's tabs with yours, so on an agent's tab, <kbd>⌥Enter</kbd> switches to the tab
too. When you're in a pin, a page from another site opens in a new tab. A
pin or Favorite row has no Split choice.

## Commands

Typing a few letters of a command's name finds the command. Commands that don't
apply to the tab you're on are left out. The commands are:

- **Workspaces:** Go to a Workspace by name, New Workspace, Rename
  Workspace, and Move this tab to….

- **Tabs:** Pin this tab, Pin this tab to this Workspace, Move this pin to
  Favorites, Add to Favorites, Copy address, Copy Markdown link, and Put
  this tab to sleep. This group also has Keep this tab awake, Let this tab sleep,
  Duplicate tab, Archive this tab, Reopen closed tab, Mute tab, Unmute tab,
  and Reader view.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Copy clean address** is in the Tabs group too. It copies the page's
  address without its tracking parameters.

</Since>

- **Splits:** Split with…, Close Split, and Swap panes.
- **The window:** Show or hide the Workspace panel.
- **The site:** Privacy and Security, Site settings, Colorshift this site,
  Clear site data, and Block JavaScript or Allow JavaScript.
- **Agents:** Give Workspace back and Agent access settings.
- **Settings:** General, Workspaces, Links, Sync and devices, Appearance,
  Search and Launcher, Privacy and protection, Sites, and Keyboard
  shortcuts settings.
- **More:** these commands open a page or a window, or act on the page
  you're on or on Sval.
  - Downloads, Extensions, and History
  - New Peek Window and New private window
  - Print, Save as PDF, Take a screenshot, and Translate page
  - Inspect page, View page source, and Color picker
  - Refresh Sval, Clear history, and Restart Sval

A command also matches other words for it. "Bookmark" finds Add to
Favorites, and "unload" finds Put this tab to sleep.

### Commands that ask you to choose

**Move this tab to…** and **Split with…** keep the Launcher open and list
your choices. Move this tab to… lists Workspaces, and Split with… lists
tabs. The command shows at the start of the field.

- Typing narrows the list.
- **Current.** This marks the Workspace the tab is already in.
- **New Workspace…** This row is last. When you've typed a name, the row
  reads New Workspace "name"… and makes a Workspace with that name.
- Backspace in the empty field, Escape, or the **Back** button takes you
  back to the first list.

## Settings › Search and Launcher › Launcher sections

Launcher sections lists the sections in the order the Launcher shows
them. The sections the Launcher doesn't show come last.

- The menu next to a section sets how many rows the section lists, from
  **None** to 10.
- **Move up** and **Move down.** The section moves up or down one place.
- **Hide.** The Launcher stops showing the section. The section moves to
  the end of the list, marked **Not shown**. Its **Show** button puts it
  back.

These limits apply while you type. When you've typed nothing, or typed %,
or you're in a command's list of choices, the Launcher lists every row
that fits. These settings sync to your other devices.

## Settings › Appearance › Address bar

- **Site name.** You see the site alone. This is the default.
- **Site and path.** You also see the path, without anything after a ? or
  #.
- **Full address.** You see the whole address after the site name.

An example under the choices shows how each one looks.

## Reader View

On a page that can be read as an article, the Reader View button shows
next to the address bar. Selecting the button, or pressing <kbd>⌥⌘R</kbd>, opens the
page in Reader View. On Windows the key is <kbd>F9</kbd>, and on Linux it's <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+R</kbd>.

## Narrow windows

As you make a window narrower, the toolbar moves its buttons into the »
menu one at a time. They go in this order: Split View, History, Downloads, Reader View,
Favorite, the site controls, Home, Reload, and Forward. The buttons work the
same from the » menu. The Workspace panel button, Back, and the address bar always stay
in the toolbar.
