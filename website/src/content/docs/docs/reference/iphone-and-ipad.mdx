Sval for iPhone and iPad syncs with Sval on your other devices through a
folder you choose, so your Workspaces and everything in them are on each
device. Sval needs iOS 26 or later on an iPhone, and iPadOS 26 or later on
an iPad.

## The bar

The bar floats at the bottom of the screen, in the color of your
Workspace.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-bar" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The bar at the bottom of a page, with Back and Forward, the shield and nps.gov, Reload, New Tab, and the Workspace button showing 6 tabs" />

- **Back and Forward.** You go back or forward in the tab's history.
  Every tab but a private one keeps its history and its place on the page
  after Sval closes.
- **The shield.** The site menu opens. See [The site menu](#the-site-menu).
- **The address.** The Launcher opens, to search the web or open an
  address in this tab. See
  [Search from the Launcher](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/search-from-the-launcher/).
- **Reload.** The page loads again. While the page loads, the button
  stops the load.
- **New Tab.** The Launcher opens for a new tab. Hold the button for
  **New Private Tab**.
- **The Workspace button.** Your Workspace opens, with its tabs. The
  button shows the Workspace's emoji and how many tabs it holds.
- **The Split button.** The button shows next to the address while a
  Split is in front and opens the Split's menu.
- **The Video and Audio button.** The controls for what's playing open
  from this button while a page plays. You can pause or stop the page,
  skip 15 seconds either way, mute the sound, or play a video in Picture
  in Picture.

As you scroll down a page the bar folds into one small pill with the
address, and it opens again when you scroll back up or tap the pill.

## Gestures

- **Hold the address and slide.** You move through the Workspace's pins and
  tabs, and lifting your finger goes to the page under it. See
  [Switch tabs by sliding on the address](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/switch-tabs-by-sliding-on-the-address/).
- **Hold the Workspace button and slide.** You move through your
  Workspaces in the same way.
- **Swipe the bar sideways.** You go to the next or the previous
  Workspace.
- **Pull down from the top of a page.** The page reloads.
- **Swipe a card sideways in the grid.** The tab closes, with Undo.

## Your Workspace

The Workspace button opens your Workspace, with its name at the top and
its pins and tabs below. The strip of every Workspace and a row of buttons
sit at the bottom.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-workspace" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The Road Trip Workspace open over a page, with its Utah group and tabs, the Workspace strip, and Settings, Favorites, and New Tab at the bottom" />

### At the top

- **The Workspace's name.** Edit Workspace opens, where you change the
  name, emoji, and color, or select **Delete This Workspace…**.
- **The Workspace options button** (three dots). The menu holds Show
  Tabs As, with **List** and **Grid**, then **Edit Tabs…**, **Edit Workspace…**, and **New Group…**.

### Tabs and groups

- **Select a tab.** The tab comes to the front, and your Workspace
  closes.
- **Hold a tab.** The tab's menu opens: **Rename…**, **Pin Tab**,
  **Add to Favorites**, **Move to…**, **Move Up**, **Move Down**,
  **Split With…**, and **Close Tab**.
- **Hold a group.** The group's menu opens: **Edit Group…**,
  **Move to…**, and **Delete Group…**.
- **Drag a tab or a card.** The tab moves, and dropping it on the middle
  of another tab makes a group or a Split. See
  [Rearrange tabs in the grid](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/rearrange-tabs-in-the-grid/).
- **A grayed tab.** The tab holds a page that Sval can't open on an
  iPhone or iPad, such as one of Sval's Settings pages. The tab stays in
  your Workspace and keeps syncing.

### The Workspace strip

- **A Workspace's tile.** Your Workspace switches to that one. Hold a
  tile for **Move Left**, **Move Right**, and **Delete Workspace…**.
- **This device's Workspace.** The tile comes first, and tabs you open
  in this Workspace show on your other devices in a Workspace named iPhone
  or iPad, after this device.
- **Devices.** Your other iPhones' and iPads' own Workspaces show under
  this heading. Each agent's Workspaces show under the agent's name.
- **New.** New Workspace opens, to name the Workspace and pick its emoji
  and color.
- **Private.** The private Workspace opens. See
  [Browse privately](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/browse-privately/).

### At the bottom

- **Settings.** Settings opens. See [Settings](#settings).
- **Favorites.** Your Favorites open, in their folders. Hold a Favorite to
  open, pin, rename, move, or delete it.
- **New Tab.** The Launcher opens for a new tab in this Workspace.

## The site menu

The shield next to the address opens the site menu for the page in front.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-site-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="The site menu for nps.gov, with Move to Workspace, Pin Tab, Add to Favorites, the Protections and JavaScript switches, Video and Audio set to Ask first, and Remove from This Site" />

- **Move to Workspace…** The tab moves to another Workspace.
- **Pin Tab.** The tab becomes a pin at the top of your Workspace.
- **Add to Favorites.** The page joins your Favorites.
- **Protections.** With Protections off, Sval doesn't block ads and
  trackers on the site.
- **JavaScript.** With JavaScript off, the site's scripts don't run.
- **Keep Links in This Tab.** With this switch on, links between the site's
  pages open in the same tab.
- **Video and Audio.** Choose **Always allow**, **Ask first**, or
  **Always deny** for the site. See
  [Allow video and audio](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/allow-video-and-audio/).
- **Remove from This Site.** Tap part of the page to hide it on every
  visit. See [Remove part of a page](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/remove-part-of-a-page/).
- **Removed from This Site.** The site's removal rules, with × to delete
  each one.
- **Zoom.** − and + zoom the site's pages out and in on this device.

Every choice but zoom syncs to your other devices. In a private tab you
can change only Protections, Video and Audio, and Zoom, and those choices
last until you exit the private Workspace.

## Settings

Settings opens from **Settings** at the bottom of your Workspace.

<Screenshot name="docs-iphone-settings" frame="rounded" maxWidth={320} alt="Settings, with Show Tabs As set to Grid, Ad and Tracker Blocking set to Balanced, and the Video and Audio choices" />

- **Show Tabs As.** Choose **List** or **Grid**. The choice applies to
  this device alone.
- **Ad and Tracker Blocking.** Choose **Off**, **Balanced**, or
  **Strict** for every site with Protections on. The level syncs to your
  other devices.
- **Video and Audio.** Choose what every site gets, and find the sites
  with a choice of their own under **Sites**. **Keep playing after you
  leave a tab** keeps video and audio playing when you switch tabs or
  leave Sval.
- **Clear Browsing Data…** Sval deletes cookies, site data, cached files,
  site icons, and tab pictures from this device, and keeps your tabs,
  history, and settings.
- **Sync.** Sval shows whether this device is syncing, and **Folder**
  names the folder this device syncs through.
  - **Sync with Another Device.** This device shows a code that another
    device types to join the folder.
  - **Leave This Folder.** This device stops syncing. See
    [Stop syncing on your iPhone or iPad](/docs/how-to/iphone-and-ipad/stop-syncing-on-your-iphone-or-ipad/).
  - **Set Up Sync…** The button shows in place of the sync status while this
    device doesn't sync, and opens setup.
- **Licenses.** Sval lists its own license and the licenses of the code
  Sval includes.
- **Privacy Policy.** Sval's privacy policy opens in a new tab.

## Downloads

A file you download shows its progress above the bar, with × to cancel
the download. When the download finishes, the file opens in a preview.
The Files app keeps your downloads in the Sval folder under On My iPhone,
or On My iPad on an iPad.

## On an iPhone turned sideways

Turned sideways, the iPhone's bar shows in one row with the Workspace's
name next to its emoji, and the Workspace opens next to the page. A Split
shows its two pages side by side.

## On an iPad

The bar and a Split keep their upright layout whichever way you hold the
iPad, and your Workspace opens over the page from the Workspace button.
With a keyboard attached, these shortcuts work:

| Shortcut | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘L</kbd> | Open the Launcher for this tab |
| <kbd>⌘T</kbd> | Open the Launcher for a new tab |
| <kbd>⌘W</kbd> | Close the tab |
| <kbd>⌘R</kbd> | Reload the page |
| <kbd>⌘[</kbd> | Go back |
| <kbd>⌘]</kbd> | Go forward |
