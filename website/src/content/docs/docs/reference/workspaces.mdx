Each Workspace keeps its own tabs and groups. A window shows one Workspace
at a time. You make, change, and remove Workspaces with the controls below.

## The switcher

<Screenshot name="home-switcher-crop" frame="crop-tl" alt="The Workspace switcher open over the Workspace panel, listing Work, Ceramics Shop and Claude" />

The switcher sits at the top of the Workspace panel. It shows the emoji, name, and
color of the Workspace you're in. That color also tints the window. The
window's title starts with the Workspace's emoji and name.

- **Isolated.** This shows next to the name when the Workspace keeps its
  own cookies and sign-ins.
- When you select the switcher, the list of Workspaces opens.
- When you right-click the switcher, the Workspace menu opens for the
  Workspace you're in.
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- When you swipe sideways over the Workspace panel, the window goes to the
  Workspace before or after the current one, in the list's order. A swipe
  skips Agent Workspaces. Settings › Keyboard shortcuts has a switch that
  turns swiping off.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- When you swipe sideways over the Workspace panel, the window goes to the
  Workspace before or after the current one, in the list's order. A swipe
  skips device Workspaces and Agent Workspaces. Settings › Keyboard shortcuts has a switch that
  turns swiping off.

</Since>


## The strip

If you choose Strip in Settings › Workspaces › Workspace switcher, the strip
takes the place of the switcher's button. It shows all your Workspaces in a
row, in the list's order. The Workspace you're in is marked as the selected
tab is, with a bar at its start.

- **A Workspace.** Clicking a Workspace switches the window to it.
  Shift-clicking it opens it in a new window.
- **The Workspace you're in.** Clicking it opens the list of Workspaces over
  the strip.
- **The keys.** When the strip has focus, Left and Right switch the window
  to the Workspace before or after the current one. Home and End switch to
  the first and the last. Enter or Space opens the list of Workspaces.
- **The mouse wheel.** The wheel slides the strip, or switches Workspaces,
  as Settings › Workspaces › Workspace switcher says.
- **The dots.** These show under the strip when your Workspaces don't fit
  in it. The long dot is the Workspace you're in. When the dots don't fit, a line takes their place, with a mark where the Workspace you're in is.
- **A box on a Workspace's icon.** The Workspace is isolated.
- **A media button on a Workspace's icon.** This shows while a tab in that
  Workspace has sound or media. Clicking it does what the media entry in
  the Workspace menu says.
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Device Workspaces.** After a line, the strip shows the Workspace each
  of your phones and tablets keeps for the tabs you open on it, each with a
  phone icon.

</Since>
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Agent Workspaces.** After a line, the strip shows the Workspaces that
  agents work in.

</Until>
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Agent Workspaces.** After a line, the strip shows the Workspaces that
  agents work in, each with its app's icon. An app with more than one
  Workspace has one icon, with a count of the app's Workspaces. Clicking
  the icon opens a list of the app's Workspaces. Each Workspace in the list
  shows the icon of the model its agent named, when Sval knows that model.
  Choosing one switches to that Workspace.

</Since>
- **New Workspace…** The plus at the end of the strip opens the New
  Workspace dialog.
- **Dragging a Workspace.** A Workspace you drag along the strip moves to
  where you drop it. Holding the drag near either end of the strip slides
  the strip that way.

When you right-click a Workspace on the strip, the Workspace menu opens for
it. For a Workspace that no window shows, the menu starts with **Open in New
Window**. Under the Workspace's commands, the menu has items that show or
hide the strip's names. The menu leaves them out when the strip is a column.

In a narrow Workspace panel, the strip shows icons only, and pointing at an
icon shows its Workspace's name. If you chose to see names, the name of the
Workspace you're in shows under the strip. When the Workspace panel is
narrower still, the strip turns into a column three Workspaces tall, with no
name, dots or line under it. Up and Down also switch Workspaces in the
column.

## The list of Workspaces

The list shows every Workspace in the same order in every window. The
Workspace you're in is in bold.

- **A Workspace.** Selecting a Workspace switches the window to it.
  Shift-clicking it, or pressing <kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd>, opens it in a new
  window. If another window already shows that Workspace, Sval brings that
  window to the front.
- **A box next to a name.** The Workspace is isolated.
- **A media button next to a name.** This shows while a tab in that
  Workspace has sound or media. Clicking the button does what the media
  entry in the Workspace menu says.
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.10">

- **Devices.** After your own Workspaces, the list shows the Workspace each
  of your phones and tablets keeps, with a phone icon and the number of tabs
  in it. You can't drag a device Workspace.

</Since>
- **Agent Workspaces.** After your own Workspaces, the list shows the
  Workspaces that agents work in.
- **New Workspace…** The New Workspace dialog opens.
- **Dragging a Workspace.** A Workspace you drag up or down the list moves
  to where you drop it. The new order syncs to your other devices. Swiping
  and the Launcher follow the new order. You can't drag an Agent Workspace.

When you point to a Workspace in the list, its submenu opens. When you
right-click a Workspace, the Workspace menu opens for it.

- **Open in New Window.** The Workspace opens in a window of its own. This
  entry shows only for a Workspace that no window shows.
- The rest of the submenu is the Workspace menu.

## The Workspace menu

<Screenshot name="docs-workspace-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={200} alt="The menu for the Work Workspace: Edit…, Isolate Workspace…, and Delete Workspace…" />

- **Pause Media.** Sval pauses the media playing in the Workspace's tabs.
  Sound that can't be paused is muted. This entry shows only while a tab
  has media or sound. It reads **Play Media**, **Mute**, or **Unmute** when
  that's what it does.
- **Edit…** The Edit Workspace dialog opens.
- **Isolate Workspace…** After you confirm, the Workspace gets its own
  cookies and sign-ins. Every tab in the Workspace reloads.
- **Stop isolating Workspace…** This shows in place of Isolate Workspace…
  for an isolated Workspace. After you confirm, every tab reloads with the
  sign-ins of your other Workspaces. Sval deletes the sign-ins and site data
  that this Workspace kept.
- **Delete Workspace…** After you confirm, Sval deletes the Workspace and
  everything in it. This entry is dimmed when you have only one Workspace.

In the menu bar, the **Workspaces** menu lists your Workspaces. Below them
is the Workspace menu for the Workspace you're in.

## The New Workspace dialog

- **Name.** This field is required.
- **Emoji.** The emoji picker opens. A new Workspace starts with a random
  emoji. **No emoji** in the picker removes the emoji.
- **Color.** This offers **Sval Default**, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green,
  Cyan, Blue, Purple, Pink, Gray, and Black. The dialog takes on the color you
  pick, as the window will.
- **Isolate this Workspace.** The new Workspace gets its own cookies and
  sign-ins. This box is unchecked unless you check it.
- **Create.** Sval makes the Workspace and switches the window to it.

## The Edit Workspace dialog

The Edit Workspace dialog has the same **Name**, **Emoji**, and **Color** as
the New Workspace dialog. They start with the Workspace's current name,
emoji, and color.

- **Archive idle tabs.** This sets how long this Workspace's tabs can go
  unused before Sval archives them: **Use Default**, 12 Hours, 24 Hours,
  7 Days, 30 Days, or **Never**. Use Default follows Settings and shows the time
  you set there.
- **Save.** Sval saves your changes.

Agent Workspaces have no Archive idle tabs choice.

## Settings › Workspaces › Workspace switcher

- **Menu.** The switcher is a button that names the Workspace you're in.
  Selecting it opens the list of Workspaces. Menu is the default.
- **Strip.** The strip shows all your Workspaces in a row.
- **The strip shows.** This sets what the strip shows: icons and names,
  icons and the current Workspace's name, or icons only. Settings shows
  this choice only while Strip is chosen.
- **Scrolling over the strip.** This sets what the mouse wheel does over
  the strip. With Slides the strip, the wheel moves the strip without
  switching Workspaces. The strip slides back when you move the pointer
  away. With Switches Workspaces, the wheel moves the window one Workspace
  at a time and stops at your first and last Workspace. Settings shows this
  choice only while Strip is chosen.

## Settings › Workspaces › Idle tabs

- **Archive idle tabs.** Sval moves tabs you haven't used for a while out
  of their Workspace and into History, under Archived tabs. This setting is
  off by default.
- **After.** This sets how long a tab can go unused: 12 Hours, 24 Hours,
  7 Days, or 30 Days. It starts at 7 Days.
- **Keep archived tabs for.** This sets how long History keeps an archived
  tab: 7, 30, or 90 days. It starts at 30 days.

These settings sync to your other devices. If you choose something other
than Use Default for a Workspace in the Edit Workspace dialog, that choice
takes the place of After for that Workspace.

## A tab's menu in the Workspace

- **Move to Workspace.** The submenu lists your other Workspaces, then
  **New Workspace…**, then any Agent Workspaces. Choosing one moves the tab,
  group, or selected rows there. Sval asks before you move tabs into or
  out of an isolated Workspace. Sval also asks before you move tabs into
  an Agent Workspace.

## History › Archived tabs

In the History sidebar, **View › Archived tabs** lists the tabs Sval
archived, newest first. Each row shows the page, the Workspace the tab came
from, and when Sval archived the tab. Selecting a row puts the tab back in
its Workspace. A tab you've put back stays in the list, dimmed.
