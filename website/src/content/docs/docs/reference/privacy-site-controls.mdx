For the site you're on, the address bar has two kinds of controls. The
Privacy and Security button shows how Sval protects you on the site. The
site controls at the end of the address bar show what the site is allowed
to do.

## The Privacy and Security button

The button sits next to the site's address.

- The number on the button counts the trackers and ads Sval blocked on the
  page. It also counts the fingerprinters Sval foiled. Over 99, the number
  reads 99+.
- When the site had a data breach in the last year, a dot shows in place
  of the number.
- When the connection isn't secure, the button shows **Not secure**. When
  Sval blocked the site as harmful, the button shows **Dangerous site**.
- Selecting the button opens the Privacy and Security panel.

## The Privacy and Security panel

<Screenshot name="home-privacy" frame="rounded" maxWidth={520} alt="The Privacy and Security panel for cnn.com, showing trackers and ads blocked and per-site protection switches" />

- The header names the site and says **Protections on** or **Protections
  off for this site**. On an address like `nas.example.com`, the site's
  name is a menu that picks that address alone or All of example.com.
- **The connection line.** The line reads, for example, **Connection
  secure**. Selecting it opens the certificate view, which shows **Verified as**, **Issued by**, **Valid**, and
  **Encryption**, then **More information**, which opens Page Info.
  **HTTPS-Only on this site** shows for a site you made an HTTPS-Only
  exception for. **Remove certificate exception** shows for a certificate
  you chose to trust.
- A warning shows first when the connection isn't secure, when parts of the
  page aren't secure, or when Sval didn't load the page.
- **This site had a data breach.** The notice shows the month, how many
  accounts were affected, and what was exposed. It names Have I Been Pwned
  as the source. Its × dismisses the notice.
- **On this page.** This counts **Trackers blocked**, **Ads blocked**, and
  **Fingerprinting foiled**. With protections off, **Allowed on this page**
  counts **Trackers loaded**.
- Under This site, both switches reload the tab:
  - **Protections for this site.** Sval blocks trackers and ads on the
    site and resists fingerprinting.
  - **Fingerprinting protection.** Turning this off lets the site read
    your device's real values. While Protections is off, this switch is off and
    you can't change it. Mozilla's compatibility fixes cover some sites.
    When those fixes are on and cover the site, this switch shows off.
    Turning the switch on keeps full protection on the site, without
    Mozilla's fix.
- At the bottom, **Manage sites** opens Settings › Sites. In a private
  window, a note says the choices you make there last until you close the
  window. They never sync.

On Sval's own pages, local files, and extension pages, the
Privacy and Security panel says that blocking and site controls don't apply.

## The site controls

From the start of the row:

- **Camera, microphone, and screen sharing.** These buttons show while the
  page uses them. The camera or microphone button mutes or unmutes that
  device. The screen sharing button opens the site settings menu, where
  **Stop** ends the sharing.
- **Location.** This button shows while the site asks for your location,
  and while the site uses your location. When the button is lit, selecting
  it blocks location on the site. When the button is crossed out, selecting
  it allows location on the site. Either way, the page reloads.
- **Video and audio.** This media button plays or stops the page's video
  and audio for this visit. When a page tries to play on a site set to Ask
  first, Sval asks whether the page can play, under this button. The button
  lights up until you answer.
- **JavaScript.** This button turns JavaScript on or off for the site and
  reloads the tab. The tooltip says whether your rule set it.
- **Scissors and eraser.** The scissors button starts Snip. The eraser
  button starts Remove from this site, and shows only once you pin it.
  Each button shows how much it removed on the page.

- **Colorshift.** This palette button opens [Colorshift](#colorshift),
  and shows only once you pin it. A dot on it shows that the site has a
  saved Colorshift.
<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Copy Clean Address.** This broom button copies the page's address
  without its tracking parameters, and shows only once you pin it. See
  [Copy a clean address](/docs/how-to/privacy/copy-a-clean-address/).

</Since>

- **Site settings.** This button opens the site settings menu. When the
  scissors or eraser button isn't pinned, its count shows on the Site
  settings button. When the Site settings button shows no count and the
  Colorshift button isn't pinned, a dot on the Site settings button shows
  that the site has a saved Colorshift.

The page's own buttons follow, after a divider:

- **Translate.** This button shows while Sval can translate the page into
  your language. While the page is translated, the button shows your
  language's code.
- **Reader View.** This button shows on an article and opens it in Reader
  View.
- **Favorite.** This button adds the page to your Favorites, or removes it.

## The site settings menu

<Screenshot name="home-site-controls-v2" alt="Site settings for bonappetit.com: zoom, element removal, Colorshift, video and audio, JavaScript, and per-site permissions" />

The header names the site and says **Site settings**. On an address like
`nas.example.com`, the site's name is a menu that picks that address alone
or All of example.com.

- **In use now.** This lists the camera, microphone, or screen the page
  uses, with **Mute**, **Unmute**, or **Stop**.
- **Zoom.** A slider shows the page's zoom and moves one step at a time.
  **Reset** puts the zoom back.

- **Colorshift.** **Colorshift This Site…** opens
  [Colorshift](#colorshift), where you change how the site looks. With
  nothing saved, the line under **Colorshift This Site…** reads "Change
  this site's colors, theme, and font". When the site has a saved
  Colorshift, the row has the same dot as the Site settings button, or as
  the Colorshift button once you pin it. The line then lists what the
  Colorshift changes, such as "Blue tint, dark theme, serif font". The pin
  next to **Colorshift This Site…** puts the Colorshift button in
  the address bar.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Address.** **Copy Clean Address** copies the page's address without
  its tracking parameters. The line under **Copy Clean Address** says
  whether the address has tracking parameters Sval knows of. The pin next to **Copy Clean Address** puts the
  Copy Clean Address button in the address bar.

</Since>

- **Elements.** **Remove from this site…** saves a rule that applies on
  every visit. **Snip…** removes something until you reload. The pin next
  to each one puts its button in the address bar. Below them, Removed from
  this site lists what you removed, with **Show again** on each item. Then
  comes **Manage in Settings**.
- **Video and audio.** This offers **Always allow**, **Ask first**, and
  **Always deny** for the site.
- **JavaScript.** There's a switch for the site, and a pin for the
  JavaScript button. Changing it reloads the tab. Turning JavaScript off
  breaks some sites.
- **Permissions.** This always lists Use the camera, Use the microphone,
  Access your location, Send notifications, and pop-ups. Then come any
  other permissions the site holds. Each permission has a menu of answers,
  and an × that clears your answer. Pop-ups show how many Sval blocked on
  the page. When you change a permission here, the tab reloads as the menu
  closes.
- **Clear cookies and site data.** Sval asks first. Then Sval clears the
  site's data from where the tab keeps it, and reloads the tab. You're
  signed out of the site.
- At the bottom, **Manage sites** opens Settings › Sites.

In private windows, Colorshift, Remove from this site, and the JavaScript
switch are off, because each of them saves a choice.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

Under **Links**, the menu has **Keep links in this tab**. The switch stops
the site from opening its own pages in new tabs or windows. A change takes
effect without a reload. The switch is off in private windows too, because
the switch saves a choice.
[Keep a site's links in the same tab](/docs/how-to/tabs/keep-a-sites-links-in-the-same-tab/)
has the details.

</Since>

## Colorshift

Colorshift changes how a site looks for you, on every visit. It opens from
the site settings menu, from the menu you get when you right-click the page
or a tab, from the Launcher, or from a key you give it. Colorshift opens over
the page. The site settings menu can be open at the same time. The page shows each change as you make it. Your saved
changes sync to all your devices. See
[Colorshift a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/colorshift-a-site/) for the steps.

- **Title.** The title names the site. Under the site's name, a line says you can
  change the site's look and feel, and that your changes sync to all your
  devices. On an address like `nas.example.com`, the site's name is a menu
  that picks that address alone or All of `example.com`. **Close** works
  as **Cancel** does. Dragging the title moves Colorshift.
- **Color wheel.** The big dot's direction picks a color, and its distance
  from the middle sets how strong the tint is. Light colors, like the page's
  background, take the big dot's color. The small dot picks the color dark
  colors take, like the page's text, and stays as far from the middle as the
  big dot. With the big dot in the middle, the page has no tint.
- **Contrast.** This moves the page's light and dark colors apart, or
  closer together.
- **Brightness.** This makes every color on the page lighter or darker.
- **Saturation.** This makes the page's colors more vivid, or grayer.

- **Theme.** This picks **Automatic**, **Light**, or **Dark**. Automatic
  lets the site follow Sval's own light or dark theme. Light and Dark ask
  the site for that theme. When the site has none, Sval turns the page's
  colors light or dark and leaves pictures and videos as they are.

- **Font.** This picks Original, System, Serif, Monospace, or Readable.
  Original keeps the site's own font.

- **Reset.** This sets every control to leave the site as it is, and the
  page shows the site's own look at once. Colorshift stays open.
  When the site has a saved Colorshift, **Save** then removes it, and
  **Cancel** keeps it.

- **Cancel.** The page goes back to how it looked when Colorshift opened,
  and Colorshift closes. Escape does the same, and so does switching tabs.
- **Save.** Sval keeps the changes for the site and closes Colorshift.
