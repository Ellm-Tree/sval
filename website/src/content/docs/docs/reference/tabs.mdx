Each Workspace keeps your tabs, groups, Splits, and pins. The Workspace
panel shows them down one side of the window. This page goes through the
Workspace from top to bottom, then its menus and settings. The switcher at
the top, which changes the Workspace, is described on
[Workspaces](/docs/reference/workspaces/).

<Screenshot name="features-panel" alt="Three Wikipedia articles in a Split, next to a Workspace of nested, color-coded tab groups" />

## Favorites

The **Favorites** button, at the bottom of the Workspace panel, opens the
Favorites menu. The menu holds all your Favorites and their folders, and
it's the same in every Workspace. A private window has no Favorites button.

When you right-click a Favorite or a folder in the menu, you get **Undo**,
**Open**, **Open in New Window**, **Open in Private Window**, and
**Open All** for a folder. The menu also has **Pin**,
**Pin to This Workspace**, **Rename…**, **Edit Address…**, and **Delete**.
The rest are **Move to**, **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **New Folder…**,
**New Separator**, and **Export…**.

- **Pin.** A copy of the Favorite or folder goes at the end of the pins
  row. The Favorite stays in the menu. Pin doesn't show for a Favorite
  whose address a pin already has.
- **Pin to This Workspace.** The copy shows in this Workspace and no other.

## The pins row

The pins row shows a tile for each pin in this Workspace, in an order you
set by dragging. The row shows only when there are pins.

- Selecting a tile shows the pin's page. The selected tile widens to show
  the pin's name. When you've moved away from the pin's saved page, the
  tile also shows the title of the page you're on.
- When you've moved away from a pin's saved page, the pin shows a dot
  while it isn't selected.
- The refresh button at the end of the selected tile takes the pin back to
  its saved page. The button is named "Back to" and the pin's name. It
  shows when you've moved away from the saved page and you point to the
  tile or focus it.

- A pin folder is one tile that shows its first few icons. Selecting the
  tile opens the pin folder's pins.
- Dropping a pin on the middle of another pin opens the Create Pin Folder
  dialog. Dropping a pin on the edge of another pin moves the pin there.
  - **Create.** Sval makes a pin folder that holds both pins. The pin
    folder is named "Untitled pin folder" unless you type a name.
  - **Cancel.** Sval makes no pin folder, and both pins stay where they
    were.
- Dropping a pin on the middle of a pin folder's tile moves the pin into
  the pin folder. Dragging a pin from the open pin folder onto the row
  takes the pin out of the pin folder.
- Right-clicking the row next to the pins shows **New Pin Folder…**. It
  asks for a name, then adds an empty pin folder at the end of the row.

- Settings › Workspaces › Pins sets how many lines of tiles show. When
  there are more pins than fit, the last tile reads "+" and the number of
  pins left over. Selecting that tile opens those pins.
- While you drag a tab and the row has no pins, the empty space reads
  **Drop to pin**.

## New tab and New group

- **New tab.** A tab opens at the top of the Workspace.
- **New group.** The New group dialog opens. The group goes in the
  Workspace or group you're working in.

## The New group and Edit group dialogs

- **Name.** This field is required.
- **Emoji.** The emoji picker opens.
- **Color.** This offers the same colors as a Workspace has. A group with a
  color shows as a tinted block.
- **Create**, or **Save** in Edit group. Sval saves the group.

## Rows

Each tab, group, and Split is a row.

- Clicking a tab's row shows its page. Clicking a group's row folds or
  unfolds the group.
- Double-clicking a row's name renames the row.
- A sleeping tab's row is dimmed, with a gray icon.
- A folded group shows how many tabs it holds. It also shows the tab you're
  viewing in that group.
- A Split's row has a tile for each tab in the Split, left to right. Clicking
  a tile shows that Split.
- A tab, or a folded group, shows a pause button while it plays sound. The
  button pauses what's playing and plays it again. When the sound can't be
  paused, you get a mute button instead.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) adds a row to the selection.
  Shift-click selects every row in between.
- Option-clicking (Alt-clicking on Windows and Linux) a tab splits it with
  the tab you're viewing.
- Pointing at a tab's row, or moving to it with the keyboard, shows a preview
  next to the Workspace panel. The preview shows the tab's title, the site,
  and a picture of the page. A tab whose page hasn't loaded since Sval started
  has no picture. The preview goes away when you move away, click, scroll, or
  press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## Dragging rows

What happens depends on where you drop a row on another row.

- **Near the top or bottom edge.** The row you drag goes before or after
  that row.
- **The middle of a tab's or Split's row.** The row fades and shows two
  halves. The half under the pointer fills with color. A drop on a half
  does what the half reads.
  - **New group**, the left half, makes a new group of the two tabs.
  - **Create Split**, the right half, makes a Split of the two. On a
    Split's row, this half reads **Join Split**. When you drag a Split,
    this half reads **Merge Splits**. On a full Split, this half reads
    "4 pages max" and is drawn faint. A drop on it does nothing.
  - A narrow Workspace panel shows only **New group**.

- **A folded group's row.** The row you drag goes inside the group, last.
  On an open group's row, the top half puts your row before the group. The
  bottom half puts your row first inside the group.
- **A dragged group or selection.** It goes before or after the row,
  depending on which half you drop it on. It never makes a group or a
  Split.

## Closing a tab

When you close the tab you're looking at, Sval shows the tab or pin in the
same Workspace that you looked at before it. Close that tab too, and Sval
goes back one more step through the tabs and pins you've looked at there.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

When a link opens a page in a new tab, Back closes the new tab and takes
you to the tab or pin that holds the link. If you've followed links in the
new tab, Back goes through those pages first.

</Since>

## The Undo message

After you close several tabs, or delete a group, a pin, or a Workspace, a
message at the bottom says what you removed.

- **Undo.** Sval puts back what you removed.
- **Dismiss.** The message closes.

The message stays for 8 seconds, and longer while the pointer is on it or
it has focus.

## The media controls

While a tab in the window has media, controls show at the bottom of the
Workspace panel for whatever played last, in any of the window's Workspaces. They are
**Previous Track**, **Back 10 Seconds**, play and pause,
**Forward 10 Seconds**, and **Next Track**. The track buttons show only on
sites that have tracks.

## The Workspace panel's edge

Dragging the edge next to the page makes the Workspace panel wider or
narrower. Double-clicking the edge puts back the default width.

## A tab's menu

<Screenshot name="docs-tab-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={216} alt="A tab’s menu in the Workspace panel: New tab, Rename, Move up dimmed, Move down, Move to group, Move to Workspace, Pin, Pin to This Workspace, Add to Favorites, Copy Markdown Link, Colorshift This Site…, Create Split, Add to Split, Put to Sleep, Keep Awake, and Close, with their shortcuts" />

- **New tab.** A new tab opens.
- **Rename.** A dialog asks for the tab's new name. The tab keeps that
  name as you browse, on every device.
- **Remove Name.** The tab shows its page's title again. This shows only
  on a tab you've renamed.
- **Move up** and **Move down**. The tab moves one row.
- **Move to group.** The submenu lists the groups the tab can go into.
  **New group…** at the end of the submenu puts the tab in a new group.
- **Move to Workspace.** The submenu lists your other Workspaces.
- **Pin.** The tab becomes a pin at the end of the pins row.
  **Pin to This Workspace** pins the tab in this Workspace and no other.
- **Add to Favorites.** Sval adds the page to Favorites. For a Favorite,
  this reads **Remove from Favorites**.
- **Copy Markdown Link.** Sval copies the page's title and address as a
  Markdown link.
- **Colorshift This Site….** Sval selects the tab and opens Colorshift for
  the tab's site.
  See [Colorshift a site](/docs/how-to/privacy/colorshift-a-site/).
- **Create Split.** A new pane opens next to the tab, where you pick a tab
  for it.
- **Add to Split.** The submenu lists the Splits in this Workspace that
  have room for the tab.
- **Put to Sleep.** Sval frees the memory the page uses. The tab stays in
  the Workspace.
- **Keep Awake.** Sval stops putting the tab to sleep. The entry is checked
  while it's on.
- **Pause Media.** This shows while the tab has media or sound. It reads
  **Play Media**, **Mute**, or **Unmute** when that's what it does.
- **Close.** The tab closes.

When you've selected several rows, only **Close**, **Move to group**,
**New group with selection**, and **Move to Workspace** work. The others
are dimmed.

## A group's menu

- **New tab.** A tab opens inside the group.
- **Edit…** The Edit group dialog opens.
- **New group.** Sval makes a group inside this group.
- **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to group**, and **Move to Workspace**.
  The group moves with everything in it.
- **Put to Sleep.** Every tab in the group goes to sleep.
- **Pause Media.** This acts on every tab in the group.

- **Delete group…** Sval asks what happens to what's inside the group. A
  group of tabs offers **Keep tabs** and **Close tabs**, and a group of
  groups offers **Keep groups** and **Delete groups**. A group with both
  offers **Keep all** and **Delete all**. A Keep button moves what's inside
  out of the group. The other button closes the tabs and deletes the groups
  with it. An empty group is deleted without asking.

## A Split pane's menu

<Screenshot name="docs-split-pane-menu" frame="rounded" maxWidth={280} alt="A Split pane’s menu: the tab’s items, then Add Tab to Split, Separate, Move Pane Left, Move Pane Right, Rename Split…, and Close Both" />

When you right-click a tile in a Split's row, you get the tab's menu
without Pin. You also get these:

- **Add Tab to Split.** A new pane opens in the Split. This is dimmed when
  the Split already has four panes.
- **Separate.** The Split turns back into ordinary tabs, in the same place.
- **Move Pane Left** and **Move Pane Right**. The pane moves one place.
  Each is dimmed when the pane is already at that end.
- **Rename Split…** This names the Split. A Split without a name
  shows the titles of its pages.
- **Close All.** Every tab in the Split closes. For a Split of two, this
  reads **Close Both**.

When you move a pane up, down, or to a group or Workspace, its whole Split
moves with it.

## A pin's menu

- **Back to saved page.** The pin goes back to its saved page.
- **Save current page as this pin.** From now on, the pin goes back to this
  page. Both entries are dimmed when you're on the saved page.
<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Show in.** This picks the Workspaces that show the pin. **Select all** and
  **Deselect all** are there too.

</Until>

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Show the pin in.** This picks the Workspaces that show the pin.
  **Select all** and **Deselect all** are there too.

</Since>

- **Move to Pin Folder.** The submenu lists the pin folders in the pins
  row, and Move to Pin Folder shows only when the row has one.
- **Rename…** This changes the pin's name.
- **Edit address…** This changes the address the pin goes back to.
- **Move to Favorites.** The pin leaves the pins row and goes to the end of
  your Favorites. Sval forgets which Workspaces you chose for the pin. If
  the pin's page is open, the page becomes a tab at the top of the list of
  tabs. You can undo this from the Undo message.
- **Delete.** Sval deletes the pin. You can undo this from the Undo
  message.

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

A pin inside a pin folder has no Show in or Move to Favorites.

</Until>

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

A pin inside a pin folder has no **Show the pin in** or **Move to Favorites**.

</Since>

When you right-click a pin folder's tile, you get these:

<Until v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Show in.** This picks the Workspaces that show the folder and its pins.

</Until>

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Show the folder in.** This picks the Workspaces that show the folder
  and its pins.

</Since>
- **Rename…** This changes the folder's name.
- **Move Contents to Row.** The folder's pins go in the pins row where the
  folder was, and Sval deletes the folder.
- **Move to Favorites.** The folder and its pins go to the end of your
  Favorites.
- **Delete.** Sval deletes the folder and its pins. You can undo this from
  the Undo message.

## A page's menu

- **Email** and an address. This shows when you right-click an email
  address you've selected on a page and the address isn't a link. A new
  message to that address opens in the app or site you've chosen for
  email.

## The Quick Peek bar

The bar at the top of a Quick Peek shows the page's address. It also shows these:

- "From" and a pin's name, when the Quick Peek came from a pin.
- **Open in Split.** The page shows in a Split, next to the tab it came
  from.
- **Open as tab.** The page stays as a tab, next to the tab it came from.
  From a pin, the page goes at the end of the Workspace, outside any group.
- The close button closes the Quick Peek, as Escape does.

## Settings › Appearance › Workspace appearance

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

- **Toolbar.** With **Above the page**, the address bar and its buttons
  sit in a row across the top of the window. This is the default. With
  **In the Workspace panel**, they sit at the top of the Workspace panel,
  and the page runs the full height of the window. See
  [Put the toolbar in the Workspace panel](/docs/how-to/tabs/put-the-toolbar-in-the-workspace-panel/).

</Since>


- **Workspace panel side.** Choose Left or Right to put the Workspace
  panel on that side of every window. Each device keeps its own choice.
- **Show a preview when you point at a tab.** While this is on, a tab's
  preview shows next to the Workspace panel. The switch is on by default.
- **Show the hidden Workspace panel when you hold the pointer at the edge
  of the window.** While the Workspace panel is hidden, it slides in over
  the page when you hold the pointer at the edge of the window on the
  Workspace panel's side. This is on by default. Each device keeps its
  own choice.
- **Workspace switcher.** Choose whether you switch Workspaces from a menu
  or from a strip. [Workspaces](/docs/reference/workspaces/) lists each
  choice.

## Settings › Workspaces › Pins

- **Rows of pins to show.** This sets how many lines of pins show: 1, 2, 3,
  4, 6, or 8. It starts at 4.

## Settings › Links › Links in pages

- **Open a link in a Quick Peek with Option-click** (Alt-click on Windows and Linux).
  Option-click on a link opens it in a Quick Peek. This is on by default. When
  it's off, Option-click does what the page expects, and the link's menu
  has no Open Link in Quick Peek.
- **Open links to other sites from a pin in a Quick Peek.** When you're in a pin
  and select a link to another site, the link opens in a Quick Peek. When this is
  off, the link opens in the pin.

## History and Downloads

- **This Workspace** and **All Workspaces.** These sit at the top of the
  History sidebar and the Downloads list. They choose whether you see the
  pages or files of this Workspace or of every Workspace.
- Under All Workspaces, each row shows its Workspace.

- **Remove from list.** This sits next to a download. The download leaves
  the list.
- **Clear list.** Every finished download the list shows leaves the list.
