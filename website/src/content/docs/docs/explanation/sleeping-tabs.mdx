A sleeping tab has given back the memory its page used. The tab keeps its
place in the Workspace and its history. When you select the tab, the page loads
again. Sval decides when tabs sleep so that the Workspaces you come back to are
as you left them, and the ones you've abandoned stop using memory.

<Screenshot name="docs-sleeping-tabs" frame="rounded" maxWidth={300} alt="The Engineering group with tokio and Pull requests dimmed as sleeping tabs, and the other tabs awake" />

## Switching Workspaces keeps pages running

When you switch a window to another Workspace, the Workspace you left keeps
running out of sight. Sval doesn't reload its pages when you come back. A
half-written Fastmail reply, a video's place, or a scrolled Codeberg diff is
where you left it.

Once a Workspace has been out of sight for three hours, Sval puts its tabs
to sleep. Three hours is long enough to come back from a meeting and find
the Workspace as it was. It's also short enough that the Workspaces you
left during the day give their memory back. If your computer runs low on
memory, Sval can put those tabs to sleep sooner.

<Since v="0.1.0-rc.11">

While [Battery Saver](/docs/how-to/tabs/use-battery-saver/) is on, Sval
puts a Workspace's tabs to sleep after the Workspace has been out of sight
for 30 minutes.

</Since>

## What stays awake

Some tabs stay awake when the three hours pass, because putting them to
sleep would lose something:

- A tab playing sound, because you can hear it.
- A tab holding text you typed and haven't sent.
- A tab in a call, or showing a picture-in-picture video.
- A tab you set to Keep Awake.

A Split sleeps as one. If one tab in a Split has to stay awake, the whole
Split stays awake, so the Split comes back whole.

## Pins

A pin's page sleeps along with the other tabs in the Workspace, and keeps
its place. When you come back, the pin shows the page it was on. When you
close a pin, or your computer runs low on memory, the pin starts again at
its saved page.

## Putting tabs to sleep yourself

You can put a tab or a group to sleep at any time, and keep a tab from ever
sleeping.
