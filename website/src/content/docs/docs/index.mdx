<Since v="ios-0.1.0">

Sval is a web browser for your Mac, Windows or Linux computer and for your
iPhone and iPad. Sval keeps your tabs in Workspaces and syncs them between
your devices through a folder you choose with no account.

</Since>

## Get started

1. [Download Sval](/download/) for each computer you use.
2. Follow [Set up Sval on two computers](/docs/tutorials/set-up-sval-on-two-computers/).
   You sync the first computer through a cloud folder, then open the same
   Workspaces on the second.
3. [Import from another browser](/docs/how-to/import-export/import-from-another-browser/)
   to bring over what you saved there.
4. [Create a Workspace](/docs/how-to/workspaces/create-a-workspace/) for each
   part of your life you want to keep apart, like work and home.

<Since v="ios-0.1.0">

5. [Set up Sval on your iPhone or iPad](/docs/tutorials/set-up-sval-on-your-iphone-or-ipad/)
   to take your Workspaces with you.

</Since>

## Features

- **[Workspaces](/docs/how-to/workspaces/create-a-workspace/).** Keep work,
  home, and other projects apart. Each window shows one Workspace, and the
  Workspace's color tints the window.
- **[Tabs in a Workspace](/docs/how-to/tabs/act-on-several-tabs-at-once/).**
  Your tabs run down one side of the window, in groups you can put inside
  other groups. Pins keep the sites you use most above your tabs.
- **[Address bar and Launcher](/docs/how-to/launcher/go-to-an-open-tab-from-the-launcher/).**
  The Launcher finds open tabs in every Workspace, runs Sval's commands, and
  searches the web.
- **[Search](/docs/how-to/search/search-the-web/).** DuckDuckGo is the
  default search engine. You can pick another, like Kagi.
- **[Links from other apps](/docs/how-to/links/open-a-peek-in-a-workspace/).**
  A link from another app opens in a Peek, a small window outside your
  Workspaces. You can keep the page as a tab in a Workspace.
- **[Sync](/docs/explanation/sync/).** Sval syncs through a folder you
  choose, like iCloud Drive or Dropbox. Sval encrypts everything it saves
  there with a key only your devices have.
<Since v="ios-0.1.0">

- **[Sval for iPhone and iPad](/docs/tutorials/set-up-sval-on-your-iphone-or-ipad/).** Your
  Workspaces sync to your iPhone and iPad. Hold the address and slide to
  switch tabs, and open private tabs that keep no history and sync nothing.

</Since>

- **[Import and export](/docs/how-to/import-export/import-from-another-browser/).**
  Bring your data from another browser, or take all of it with you.
- **[Privacy and blocking](/docs/explanation/what-sval-keeps-private/).** Sval
  blocks ads and trackers and works against fingerprinting. You choose how
  much it blocks, and you can change that for any site.
- **[Passwords](/docs/how-to/passwords/choose-your-password-manager/).** Your
  passwords stay in the password manager you already use.
- **[Extensions](/docs/how-to/extensions/install-an-extension/).** Sval runs
  extensions from addons.mozilla.org, including full-strength ad blockers
  like uBlock Origin.
- **[Agents](/docs/explanation/agents/).** AI apps on your computer can use
  Sval, each in its own Workspace, under rules you set.
- **[Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/shortcuts/).** See every shortcut on
  macOS, Windows, and Linux. You can change the ones you want.
- **[Updates](/docs/how-to/updates/install-an-update/).** Sval checks for
  updates once a day and asks you to restart when one is ready.
- **[Troubleshooting](/docs/how-to/troubleshooting/send-feedback/).** Send
  feedback or a crash report, or find Sval's log file.
- **[Source code](/docs/explanation/source-code/).** Sval's source code is
  public on GitLab. You can report a problem there, or send a change as a
  merge request.
